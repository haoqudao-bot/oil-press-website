One of the most overlooked — yet critically important — aspects of purchasing an **oil press machine** is ensuring it is compatible with your local electrical supply. Voltage mismatches can damage your machine, void your warranty, and cost thousands in repairs. This guide covers everything you need to know about **oil press machine voltage** requirements, global power standards, generator sizing, and how Haonuo customizes every machine for your specific electrical environment.

## Why Voltage Matters for Oil Press Machines

Oil press machines use electric motors to drive the hydraulic pump system. The motor is designed to operate at a specific voltage and frequency. Operating a motor at the wrong voltage or frequency causes several problems:

**Undervoltage (lower voltage than rated)** — The motor draws more current to compensate, causing overheating, reduced torque, and potentially burning out the motor windings. Even a 10% voltage drop can reduce motor life by 50%.

**Overvoltage (higher voltage than rated)** — Excess voltage causes increased current flow, insulation breakdown, and premature motor failure. Overvoltage is less common but equally damaging.

**Frequency mismatch** — A motor designed for 50 Hz running on 60 Hz power (or vice versa) will run at a different speed. A 50 Hz motor on 60 Hz power runs 20% faster, which can overload the hydraulic pump. A 60 Hz motor on 50 Hz power runs 17% slower, reducing hydraulic pump output and press performance.

**The bottom line**: Always ensure your oil press machine is configured for your exact local voltage and frequency. Haonuo customizes every machine at the factory — at no extra charge — to match your specific power supply.

## Generator Sizing Guide

In many regions, especially in Africa and South Asia, the electrical grid is unreliable or unavailable. A backup generator is essential for consistent production. Here is how to size your generator correctly:

### Sizing Formula

Generator size (KVA) = Motor power (KW) × Starting factor × Safety margin ÷ Power factor

- **Starting factor**: Hydraulic motors have a starting surge of 2-3 times running current
- **Safety margin**: Add 20-30% for other loads (lighting, filter press, etc.)
- **Power factor**: Typically 0.8 for generators

**Important tips for generator operation:**
- Always start the generator and let it stabilize before turning on the machine
- Never run the generator at more than 80% of its rated capacity for extended periods
- Use a voltage regulator (AVR) to protect the machine from voltage fluctuations
- Diesel generators are more fuel-efficient and reliable than petrol generators for continuous operation

## Three-Phase vs Single-Phase Power

### Single-Phase Power (220V-240V)

Single-phase power is the standard residential and small commercial supply in most countries. It uses two wires (live and neutral) and is available virtually everywhere.

**Advantages:**
- Available in any location with electricity
- Compatible with small generators
- Simpler wiring and lower installation cost

**Limitations:**
- Limited power capacity (typically up to 5-7 KW)
- Motors may have slightly lower efficiency than three-phase equivalents

Haonuo's hydraulic oil presses (HN355, HN426, HN500) all operate on single-phase 220V power with only 2.2 KW motor requirement, making them ideal for locations with only single-phase supply.

### Three-Phase Power (380V-415V)

Three-phase power uses three live wires plus a neutral and is the standard for industrial and large commercial installations. It delivers more power more efficiently.

**Advantages:**
- Higher power capacity
- More efficient motor operation
- Smoother motor running with less vibration
- Required for larger equipment (screw presses, complete lines)

**Limitations:**
- Not available in all locations
- Higher installation cost
- Requires a three-phase generator (more expensive)

**Recommendation**: If three-phase power is available at your location, use it for the screw pre-press expeller (HN-CG60) and any larger equipment. For the hydraulic press alone, single-phase 220V is perfectly adequate.

## Electrical Safety and Grounding

Proper electrical safety is essential for operator protection and machine longevity:

### Grounding

Every oil press machine must be properly grounded (earthed). This protects operators from electric shock in case of insulation failure. Connect the machine's grounding terminal to a proper earth ground — a metal rod driven into the ground, or the building's grounding system.

### Circuit Protection

Install appropriate circuit breakers and fuses between the power supply and the machine:

- **HN355/HN426/HN500 (2.2 KW)**: 16A circuit breaker, 220V
- **HN-CG60 (7.5 KW)**: 25A circuit breaker, 380V three-phase
- **HN-10SL Filter Press (0.75 KW)**: 10A circuit breaker, 220V

### Overload Protection

Haonuo machines include built-in thermal overload protection that automatically shuts off the motor if it overheats. Never bypass this protection — it prevents motor damage and fire risk.

### Wet Environment Precautions

Oil pressing involves water and oil, creating potential electrical hazards. Keep all electrical connections, control panels, and junction boxes dry and protected from splashing. Use IP54 or higher rated enclosures for electrical components in wet environments.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use a 220V machine on 110V power?**
No. A 220V motor on 110V power will not operate correctly — it will draw excessive current, overheat, and eventually burn out. If you only have 110V power, you need a step-up transformer (110V to 220V, minimum 5 KVA for the hydraulic press). Alternatively, Haonuo can supply a machine with a 110V motor, though this is a special configuration. Contact us for details.

**What happens if I run a 50 Hz machine on 60 Hz power?**
A 50 Hz motor on 60 Hz power will run approximately 20% faster. For a hydraulic oil press, this means the hydraulic pump delivers more flow, which can cause the press to cycle faster than designed. While this may not immediately damage the machine, it can cause premature wear on seals and hydraulic components. We strongly recommend ordering a machine configured for your exact frequency.

**Do I need three-phase power for the hydraulic press?**
No. All Haonuo hydraulic oil presses (HN355, HN426, HN500) operate on single-phase 220V power with only 2.2 KW motor requirement. Three-phase power is only needed for the screw pre-press expeller (HN-CG60, 7.5 KW) or complete production lines with multiple machines.

**How do I know what voltage I have?**
You can check your voltage using a multimeter or by looking at your electrical panel. In most of Africa, Asia, and Europe, the standard is 220V-240V / 50 Hz. In the Americas, it is typically 110V-120V / 60 Hz. If you are unsure, ask a local electrician to measure your supply voltage and frequency. When you order from Haonuo, simply tell us your country and we will configure the machine correctly.

**Can the machine handle voltage fluctuations?**
Haonuo's machines are designed to tolerate ±10% voltage variation from the rated voltage. For example, a 220V machine will operate normally between 198V and 242V. If your power supply has fluctuations beyond this range, we recommend installing a voltage stabilizer (automatic voltage regulator / AVR) between the power supply and the machine. This is especially important for generator-powered operations where voltage can vary significantly with load changes.
