## India's Mustard Oil Surge: Opportunity for Hydraulic Press Buyers

India's edible oil landscape is transforming rapidly in 2026, creating significant opportunities for hydraulic oil press machine buyers and operators.

### Market Overview

India's edible oil imports reached **$49.7 billion** in FY2025-26, making it the world's largest edible oil importer. However, a powerful shift toward domestic production is underway:

- The **National Mission on Edible Oils (NMEO)** has allocated ₹11,000 crore to boost domestic oilseed production
- **Mustard/rapeseed acreage** has increased 12% in the 2025-26 rabi season
- The **Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan (PM-AASHA)** ensures MSP support for oilseed farmers

### Why This Matters for Oil Press Buyers

**1. Kachi Ghani Premium is Growing**

Traditional cold-pressed mustard oil (kachi ghani) commands a **30-50% price premium** over refined mustard oil in Indian markets. Consumers increasingly prefer kachi ghani for its natural flavor and perceived health benefits, following Ayurvedic traditions.

Hydraulic oil presses are the **ideal technology** for kachi ghani production because:
- They operate at low temperatures (below 50°C), preserving the natural pungency
- Batch pressing matches traditional production methods
- The thick, aromatic oil matches consumer expectations for "real" kachi ghani

**2. Small-Scale Mill Renaissance**

India is seeing a revival of small-scale oil mills (ghanis) modernized with hydraulic presses:
- **Rural entrepreneurs** are setting up 5-20 kg/batch operations
- **Farmer cooperatives** are investing in shared pressing facilities
- **Urban specialty stores** are sourcing directly from local presses

**3. Quality Regulations Favoring Cold-Pressed**

FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) is tightening labeling requirements:
- "Cold-pressed" claims now require documented temperature control
- Adulteration penalties have increased 3x
- This favors legitimate hydraulic press operators over informal screw press setups

### Looking Ahead

With India targeting 50% self-sufficiency in edible oil by 2030, the demand for oil pressing equipment — particularly hydraulic presses for premium cold-pressed oil — will continue growing. Early entrants who establish quality brands and distribution networks now will have the strongest position as the market matures.
