## What Affects Hydraulic Oil Press Pricing

Understanding the factors that influence pricing helps you evaluate quotes from different suppliers and avoid overpaying.

### 1. Machine Size and Capacity

Larger machines with bigger pressing chambers and more powerful hydraulic systems naturally cost more. However, the price-per-kilogram of capacity decreases as you go up in size, making larger models more cost-effective per unit of output. The HN500 delivers significantly more capacity than the HN325 at a proportionally lower cost per kg/batch — a significant economy of scale.

### 2. Customization Requirements

Custom features that affect pricing include:

- **Voltage customization** — Haonuo offers this at no extra charge, but some suppliers may charge extra
- **Stainless steel contact parts** — Food-grade stainless steel for oil-contact surfaces
- **Automatic cake discharge** — Semi-automatic ejection system
- **Digital pressure display** — Upgraded control panel
- **Custom color/branding** — OEM painting and labeling

**Customization options are quoted individually based on your requirements — tell us what you need and we'll prepare a detailed breakdown.**

### 3. Shipping and Logistics

Shipping is often the most variable cost component. **Shipping costs vary by destination and current freight rates — request a CIF quote for your port.**

Factors that affect shipping costs:
- Destination port
- Machine model and weight
- Current sea freight rates
- Seasonal demand and port congestion
- Whether you choose FOB, CIF, or other Incoterms

**Note:** Air freight is available but typically costs 3–5x more than sea freight. Shipping costs fluctuate based on fuel prices, seasonal demand, and port congestion.

### 4. Import Duties and Taxes

Your local import duties and taxes can significantly increase the landed cost. **Import duties vary by country and HS code classification — we recommend consulting a local customs broker for accurate rates.**

Oil press machines are typically classified under HS code 8479.82 (machines for extracting or preparing animal or vegetable fats or oils). Duty rates and applicable taxes vary significantly by country. Always check with a local customs broker for current rates, as these change frequently.

## Common Questions About Hydraulic Oil Press Machine Prices

### Why do prices vary so much between suppliers?

Price variation comes from three main sources: (1) **Trading company vs. manufacturer** — Trading companies add margins of 20–50%. (2) **Quality differences** — Cheaper machines may use thinner steel, lower-grade hydraulic components, or less precise machining, which reduces machine life and oil yield. (3) **What is included** — Some quotes exclude essential components like the hydraulic pump, filter cloths, or control panel.

### Is it cheaper to buy from a local distributor?

Sometimes, but not always. Local distributors offer the convenience of local stock and service, but their prices are typically 30–60% higher than factory-direct because they need to cover their import costs, warehousing, and profit margin. Buying factory-direct from Haonuo and handling local installation yourself is usually the most cost-effective approach.

### Can I negotiate the price?

Yes, especially for orders of 2+ machines or when purchasing a complete setup (press + roaster + filter). Haonuo offers volume discounts depending on order size. We also occasionally run promotional pricing — [contact us](/contact) to ask about current offers.

### Are there hidden costs I should know about?

The most commonly overlooked costs are: (1) **Import duties and taxes** — Can add a significant percentage to the CIF value depending on your country. (2) **Port handling and clearance fees** — Varies by port. (3) **Inland transportation** — From the port to your facility. (4) **Electrical installation** — If you need a dedicated circuit or transformer. (5) **Initial spare parts** — Budget for filter cloths, seals, and hydraulic oil.
