## Why Hydraulic Oil Press is Ideal for African Markets

The hydraulic oil press is the most popular type of oil press machine in Africa for several compelling reasons:

**Durability and Simplicity** — Hydraulic oil presses have few moving parts compared to screw presses. The hydraulic system is robust and can operate for years with minimal maintenance. This is crucial in Africa where access to specialized repair technicians may be limited. A hydraulic press can be maintained by a mechanically-inclined operator with basic tools.

**Affordability** — Hydraulic oil presses offer excellent value for money. A complete setup (press + filter) from Haonuo is significantly less than industrial screw press systems, making it accessible to small and medium entrepreneurs. Request current pricing for your preferred model.

**Versatility** — One hydraulic press can process a wide range of oilseeds common in Africa: groundnut (peanut), palm kernel, sesame, shea butter, cottonseed, sunflower, and more. This versatility allows you to switch between raw materials based on seasonal availability and market prices.

**Low Power Consumption** — Haonuo's hydraulic presses require only 2.2 KW of motor power, making them compatible with small generators (5-7 KVA) commonly used in areas with unreliable electricity. This is a critical advantage over screw presses that typically require 7.5-15 KW.

**High Oil Yield** — Hydraulic presses achieve residual oil rates of 5-8% in the pressed cake, meaning you extract more oil from every kilogram of raw material. This directly translates to higher revenue.
