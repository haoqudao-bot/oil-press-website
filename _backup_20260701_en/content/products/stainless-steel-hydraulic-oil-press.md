## Overview

The HN325S Stainless Steel Hydraulic Oil Press is the food-grade variant of our popular HN325 Standard model, featuring food-grade stainless steel construction for the barrel and all oil-contact surfaces. Designed specifically for export markets and premium oil brands that require strict food safety compliance, the HN325S uses FDA-compliant food contact materials consistent with international food safety standards including EU regulations. Note: Our machines have not been separately certified or approved by the FDA. It delivers the same reliable 325-ton pressing performance as the standard model while ensuring the highest level of hygiene and material safety.

With 325 tons of pressing force, 60 Mpa system pressure, and 27.78 Mpa/cm² cake surface pressure, the HN325S handles all conventional oil seeds with ease. The stainless steel barrel and contact surfaces prevent contamination, corrosion, and metal leaching, making this machine the ideal choice for producers targeting premium markets, organic certification, and international export.
