## Overview

The 27 KW Electric Seed Steamer is a completely upgraded model featuring a thickened dual water tank design — the main tank handles heating while the auxiliary tank uses negative-pressure water injection for safe, reliable operation. Both tanks and all connecting pipes are constructed from 304 stainless steel for corrosion resistance and food-grade safety. With a maximum capacity of 100 kg per batch and a 20-minute full-steam cycle, this steamer delivers efficient, consistent seed conditioning for oil pressing operations.

Safety and maintainability are at the core of this design: electrical and water sections are physically separated, the control panel offers 3-level adjustable heating with an emergency stop switch, and a quick-open electrical inspection port enables fast servicing. The separable pot body design makes discharging easy, while dedicated drain ports on both tanks simplify scale removal.

## Key Advantages

- **Dual water tank system** — main tank for heating + auxiliary negative-pressure tank for water injection, ensuring continuous safe operation
- **304 stainless steel construction** — tanks, pipes, and fittings all food-grade stainless for durability and hygiene
- **Electrical-water separation** — physically partitioned compartments for maximum operator safety
- **3-level adjustable heating** — 9 / 18 / 27 KW stages for precise temperature control plus emergency stop switch
- **Quick-open electrical inspection port** — fast access for maintenance without disassembly
- **Separable pot body** — lift-off design for easy discharging; pot nests into water basin with steam-sealed fit
- **Independent water float** — installed in auxiliary tank for low failure rate, with dedicated quick-repair replacement port
- **Drain ports on both tanks** — convenient scale cleaning for long-term reliable performance
- **100 kg per batch, 20-minute cycle** — fast throughput for continuous production

## Applications & Suitable Materials

This electric steamer is designed for pre-press seed steaming and conditioning:

- **Tea seeds** — thorough steaming for optimal pressing moisture
- **Rapeseed** — consistent conditioning for high oil yield
- **Peanuts** — steam softening before pressing
- **Sesame** — gentle steaming preserves oil quality
- **Mustard seed** — pre-press moisture adjustment

Ideal for small-to-medium oil pressing workshops, especially those with reliable electrical supply seeking automated, hands-off steaming operation.
