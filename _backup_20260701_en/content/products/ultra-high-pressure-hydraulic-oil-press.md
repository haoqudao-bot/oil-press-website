## Overview

The HN500 Ultra High Pressure Hydraulic Oil Press is the flagship of our product line, built for the most demanding oil extraction challenges. Featuring a massive 5 cm thick steel plate frame — the heaviest in our range — it delivers a staggering 620-ton pressing force with 52 Mpa/cm² cake surface pressure. This is the machine for operators who need maximum oil yield from the toughest cold-press materials.

Whether you are cold pressing walnut kernels, in-shell walnuts, apricot kernels, yellowhorn, or tiger nuts, the HN500 extracts every last drop in a single cycle. Its exceptional pressure also means conventional oil seeds achieve competitive residual oil rates, directly improving your bottom line.

## Key Advantages

- **Maximum Cake Pressure** — 52 Mpa/cm² is the highest cake surface pressure in our standard series, ensuring complete oil extraction from even the most challenging materials.
- **620-Ton Pressing Force** — The most powerful hydraulic oil press in our lineup, capable of handling any cold-press or conventional material with ease.
- **5 cm Steel Plate Frame** — The thickest frame in our range provides exceptional structural integrity for ultra-high-pressure operation and long service life.
- **Single-Cycle Cold Press** — Hard-shell and cold-press materials are fully extracted in one cycle — no re-pressing needed, saving time and maximizing throughput.
- **Lowest Residual Oil** — The extreme pressure leaves the least possible oil in the cake, maximizing your yield and revenue from every batch.
- **Dual-Barrel Productivity** — Double-barrel configuration with dual sliding rails enables continuous operation for maximum daily output.

## Core Technology

- **Bar-Type Slotted Barrel** — Haonuo's proprietary slotted barrel design provides significantly more oil extraction area than traditional drilled barrels. Oil flows faster and clearer, with approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary) — essential for ultra-high-pressure extraction where maximum yield is the priority. No clogging, no cleaning between batches.
- **German Hydraulic System** — Ultra-high pressure combined hydraulic pump station with high-low dual pump. Working pressure up to 75 Mpa with rapid cylinder return — the highest system pressure in our lineup.
- **4-Hook Cake Removal** — Innovative 4-hook mechanism ensures easy, reliable cake discharge — even after overnight pressing of the toughest materials.
- **High Carbon Steel Frame** — High-frequency quenched and heat-treated steel for long-lasting service. Actual service life depends on operating conditions and maintenance under 620-ton pressing force.
- **Microcomputer Control** — Automated pressing cycle from loading to finished oil in minutes.
- **Energy Efficient** — Designed to reduce power consumption compared to previous-generation presses (actual savings vary by operating conditions).
- **Labor Efficient** — Only 1-2 operators needed, reducing labor requirements compared to manual pressing methods.
