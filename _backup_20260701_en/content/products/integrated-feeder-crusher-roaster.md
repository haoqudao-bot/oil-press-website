## Overview

The Integrated Feeder Crusher Roaster is an all-in-one processing unit that combines automatic feeding, crushing, and roasting into a single machine group — eliminating the need for material transfer between separate pieces of equipment. Designed for small-to-medium oil pressing operations, this integrated system streamlines your production line from raw seed input to roast-ready output in one continuous flow.

By consolidating three essential preprocessing steps into one compact footprint, this machine reduces labor requirements, minimizes material handling losses, and significantly improves overall production efficiency. Custom configurations are available to match your specific seed types and throughput requirements.

## Key Advantages

- **Three-in-one functionality** — feeding, crushing, and roasting in a single integrated system
- **Reduced labor costs** — automated material flow eliminates manual transfer between machines
- **Space-saving design** — one compact unit replaces three separate machines, freeing up workshop floor space
- **Continuous processing** — raw seeds go in, fully processed material comes out without interruption
- **Lower material loss** — no transfer spills or handling waste between processing stages
- **Custom configurations** — capacity and settings adjustable for different seed types and production volumes
- **Quick ROI** — reduced equipment investment, lower labor costs, and higher efficiency accelerate payback

## Applications & Suitable Materials

This integrated system is ideal for oil seed processing lines where space and labor efficiency are priorities:

- **Tea seeds** — complete feed-crush-roast preprocessing
- **Rapeseed (canola)** — high-throughput continuous processing
- **Peanuts** — from whole nut to roast-ready in one pass
- **Sunflower seeds** — efficient hull-in or dehulled processing
- **Other oil seeds** — sesame, flaxseed, and similar materials

Best suited for small-to-medium oil pressing workshops, cooperative processing facilities, and operations looking to simplify their preprocessing workflow.
