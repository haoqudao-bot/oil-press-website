## Overview

The HN325 Standard Hydraulic Oil Press is our popular hydraulic oil press machine, trusted by oil producers worldwide for its outstanding value and reliable performance. As the standard extended-stroke model in our lineup, it features an 850 mm cylinder stroke that presses all the way to the bottom, handling batch sizes from 5 to 100 kg with ease. Whether you are a small-scale producer or running a mid-size operation, the HN325 delivers consistent oil extraction results across a wide range of materials.

The HN325 cylinder paired with a 390 mm inner diameter barrel achieves a cake surface pressure of 27.78 Mpa/cm², enabling most conventional oil seeds to be fully pressed in a single cycle. This can contribute to higher oil yield and lower residual oil in the cake. Actual return on investment depends on many factors including market conditions and operating efficiency.

- **Labor Efficient** — Only 1-2 operators needed, reducing labor requirements compared to manual pressing methods.
