## Overview

The 220 Type High Speed Seed Crusher is a professional-grade oil seed preprocessing machine built around a flywheel-style structural design. Engineered for hard-shell seed crushing, it features interchangeable screen meshes that allow operators to adjust output particle size on demand — with the finest mesh capable of producing micron-level particles. This crusher is an essential preprocessing step before hydraulic oil pressing, ensuring optimal oil yield from tough, hard-shelled raw materials.

With a throughput of 750 kg/h and a robust 11 KW motor running at 1460 RPM, this machine delivers consistent, high-volume crushing performance for commercial oil pressing operations of all sizes.

## Key Advantages

- **Flywheel-style crushing mechanism** — delivers powerful, uniform impact force for efficient hard-shell material breakdown
- **Interchangeable screen meshes** — quickly swap screens to produce different particle sizes, from coarse to micron-level fineness
- **Shell-on processing capability** — crush tea seeds, yellowhorn, avocado seeds and other hard-shelled materials without prior de-shelling
- **High throughput** — 750 kg/h processing capacity keeps up with demanding production schedules
- **Low noise operation** — only ~82 dB, suitable for continuous workshop use
- **Compact footprint** — 1400×802×800 mm dimensions fit easily into existing production lines

## Applications & Suitable Materials

The 220 Type is specifically designed for hard-shell oil seeds that are difficult to process with standard crushers:

- **Tea seeds (camellia seeds)** — with or without shell
- **Yellowhorn (Xanthoceras sorbifolium)** — hard-shell variety
- **Avocado seeds** — dense, tough pit material
- **Other hard-shell seeds and materials** — walnuts, palm kernels, and similar

This crusher is the ideal preprocessing solution before hydraulic pressing of hard-shell seeds, significantly improving oil extraction efficiency.
