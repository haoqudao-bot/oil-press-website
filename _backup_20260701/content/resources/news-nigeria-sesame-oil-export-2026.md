## Nigeria's Sesame Oil Boom: A Growing Market for Hydraulic Presses

Nigeria is emerging as one of Africa's most exciting markets for cold-pressed sesame oil production, with export revenues surging and domestic processing capacity racing to keep up.

### Export Growth

Nigeria's sesame oil export revenue reached **$1.72 billion** in Q1 2026, an **86% increase** over the same period in 2025. Key drivers:

- **Global health food demand** — Cold-pressed sesame oil is increasingly used in organic food products, cosmetics, and pharmaceuticals in Europe, North America, and the Middle East
- **Nigeria's sesame quality advantage** — Nigerian sesame (particularly the white variety from Kebbi and Benue states) is prized for its high oil content (50-55%) and mild flavor
- **NAQS certification improvements** — The National Agricultural Quarantine Service has streamlined phytosanitary certification, making exports smoother

### Processing Capacity Gap

Despite the export surge, **Nigeria processes only 15% of its sesame domestically**. The remaining 85% is exported as raw seed, losing significant value-add:

| Product | FOB Value/kg | Value Add |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| Raw sesame seed | $1.20 | Baseline |
| Cold-pressed sesame oil | $4.50-6.00 | **275-400%** |
| Refined sesame oil | $3.00-4.00 | 150-233% |

This gap represents a massive opportunity for Nigerian entrepreneurs who invest in oil pressing equipment.

### Why Hydraulic Presses Are the Right Choice

For Nigerian sesame oil targeting export markets, **hydraulic oil presses offer critical advantages**:

1. **Cold-pressed certification** — European and North American buyers specifically request cold-pressed oil (pressed below 50°C). Hydraulic presses naturally operate at these temperatures; screw presses require active cooling systems.

2. **Oil quality** — Hydraulic presses produce lighter-colored, milder-flavored oil that matches export market preferences for "virgin" sesame oil.

3. **Batch traceability** — Export customers increasingly require batch-level traceability. Hydraulic press batch production naturally supports this; continuous screw press output is harder to segregate by batch.

4. **Lower initial investment** — For Nigerian entrepreneurs entering the market, a hydraulic press setup costs significantly less than a screw press line of equivalent quality output.

Consider a sesame farming cooperative in Kebbi State that might install **3× HN500 hydraulic presses** with supporting roasters and filter presses. A setup like this could potentially process:

- Approximately **1,500 kg sesame/day** into cold-pressed oil
- Target export contracts with European organic food distributors
- Potential value increase from raw seed to cold-pressed oil
- Creation of local jobs in the community

*(Note: This is an illustrative example only. Actual results vary significantly based on raw material quality, market conditions, operator skill, and many other factors.)*

### Market Outlook

With the Nigerian government's Agricultural Transformation Agenda prioritizing sesame value-addition, and global cold-pressed oil demand projected to grow 12% annually through 2030, the opportunity window for hydraulic press investment in Nigeria is wide open.
