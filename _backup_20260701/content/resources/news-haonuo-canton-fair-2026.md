## Haonuo Machinery at the 137th Canton Fair

From April 15 to May 5, 2026, Haonuo Machinery participated in the 137th China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair), showcasing our complete hydraulic oil press machine series at Phase 1 — Electronics & Electrical Equipment.

### Booth Highlights

Our booth featured live pressing demonstrations with sesame and peanut seeds, allowing visitors to see the cold-pressed oil quality firsthand. Key models on display:

- **HN500 Commercial Hydraulic Press** — The most popular model for established businesses, capable of 35-50 kg/batch with consistent pressure control
- **HN355 Stainless Steel Edition** — New for 2026, featuring food-grade 316 stainless steel contact surfaces for markets with strict hygiene requirements
- **HN426 with Integrated Roaster** — A complete pressing station for businesses processing multiple seed types

### Visitor Response

Over the 20-day exhibition, we welcomed **230+ serious buyer discussions** from 32 countries, with the strongest interest from:

- **West Africa** (Nigeria, Ghana) — Cold-pressed sesame and groundnut oil
- **South Asia** (India, Pakistan) — Mustard/kachi ghani oil production
- **Southeast Asia** (Indonesia, Philippines) — Virgin coconut oil
- **Middle East** — Sesame and black seed oil

### Key Takeaways

1. **Cold-pressed oil demand continues to grow** — More buyers are specifically seeking hydraulic presses for premium oil quality, moving away from screw press alternatives
2. **Stainless steel is increasingly required** — Food safety regulations in target markets are driving demand for 304/316 stainless steel contact surfaces
3. **Complete solutions outsell standalone machines** — Buyers prefer integrated pressing stations (press + roaster + filter) over individual machines
