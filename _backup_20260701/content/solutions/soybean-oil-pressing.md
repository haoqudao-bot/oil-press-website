### Soybean Oil Market Opportunities

- **Cooking Oil**: The largest market segment. Refined soybean oil is a staple cooking oil worldwide.
- **Cold-Pressed Premium Oil**: Growing demand from health-conscious consumers who prefer unrefined, non-GMO soybean oil.
- **Industrial Applications**: Soybean oil is used in biodiesel production, printing inks, and industrial lubricants.
- **Soybean Meal**: The press cake is a high-protein animal feed ingredient, often worth 60-70% of the total revenue.
