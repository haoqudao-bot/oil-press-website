A dependable **mustard oil press machine** is the foundation of any mustard oil production business, whether you produce traditional Kachi Ghani cold-pressed oil or refined mustard oil for the mass market.

## Oil Yield Expectations

| Raw Material | Oil Content | Expected Press Yield | Residual Oil Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow Mustard Seeds | 35-40% | 30-36% | ≤ 5% |
| Brown Mustard Seeds | 35-42% | 30-38% | ≤ 5% |
| Black Mustard Seeds | 35-40% | 28-35% | ≤ 6% |
| Steamed Mustard Seeds | 35-42% | 33-39% | ≤ 4% |

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Seed variety**: Brown mustard (Brassica juncea) typically yields more oil than black mustard
- **Steaming**: Pre-steaming increases yield by 3-5% and adjusts the pungency level
- **Moisture content**: Optimal pressing moisture is 5-7%
- **Pressing method**: Cold pressing (Kachi Ghani) yields slightly less but produces premium oil

## Recommended Process

1. **Seed Cleaning** — Remove dust, stones, and other impurities from mustard seeds. Clean seeds produce better-tasting oil and protect your equipment from damage.
2. **Steaming (For Hot Press)** — Steam mustard seeds at 90-100°C for 15-20 minutes using the Electric Seed Steamer. This softens the seeds, increases oil yield, and reduces the sharp pungency. Skip for Kachi Ghani cold-pressed oil.
3. **Cooling** — Allow steamed seeds to cool to 50-70°C before pressing. Proper temperature ensures optimal oil flow without degrading quality.
4. **Hydraulic Pressing** — Feed prepared mustard seeds into the Standard or Heavy Duty Hydraulic Oil Press. Each batch of 25-50 kg processes in 8-12 minutes.
5. **Filtration** — Pass crude mustard oil through the Pneumatic Oil Filter Press to remove seed particles and sediment.
6. **Settling** — Allow filtered oil to settle for 24-48 hours. Mustard oil benefits from extended settling to develop its characteristic flavor.
7. **Packaging** — Bottle the finished mustard oil. Traditional tin containers or PET bottles are common in South Asian markets.

**What is Kachi Ghani mustard oil?**
Kachi Ghani is the traditional Indian term for cold-pressed mustard oil, extracted without heat or chemicals. It retains the natural pungent flavor, aroma, and nutritional properties of mustard seeds. Kachi Ghani oil commands a premium price in the Indian market.

**Is mustard oil pressing a profitable business?**
Yes, it can be. Mustard oil is consumed daily by hundreds of millions of people in South Asia. India alone produces over 8 million tons of mustard oil annually.
