## Overview

The HN-CG60 Screw Pre-Press Expeller utilizes a screw-type crushing principle that delivers quiet, stable operation with virtually no wear parts — translating to exceptionally low maintenance costs over the machine's lifetime. Designed for soft to medium-hardness oil seeds, this expeller efficiently breaks down rapeseed, soybeans, flaxseed, and other common oil-bearing materials, making it an indispensable preprocessing machine for oil pressing lines.

Available in two power configurations — the standard 7.5 KW model with 300–350 kg/h output, and an 11 KW upgrade delivering 750 kg/h — the HN-CG60 adapts to your production scale without requiring a different machine platform.

## Key Advantages

- **Screw-type crushing principle** — gentle yet effective, preserving oil content while achieving thorough seed breakdown
- **Ultra-low noise** — significantly quieter than hammer-mill or flywheel-type crushers, improving workshop comfort
- **Stable, consistent operation** — smooth continuous feeding and crushing with minimal vibration
- **No wear parts** — screw mechanism design eliminates high-wear components, reducing downtime and replacement costs
- **Dual power options** — choose 7.5 KW (300–350 kg/h) or 11 KW (750 kg/h) to match your throughput needs
- **Low total cost of ownership** — minimal maintenance + low energy consumption = fast ROI

## Applications & Suitable Materials

The HN-CG60 excels at crushing soft to medium-hardness oil seeds:

- **Rapeseed (canola)** — high-oil-content seed, ideal pre-press material
- **Soybeans** — consistent crushing for optimal pressing results
- **Flaxseed (linseed)** — small seed, efficient throughput
- **Sichuan pepper seeds** — specialty spice seed oil extraction
- **Sesame** — delicate seed handled gently to preserve oil quality
- **Walnut kernels** — soft, high-oil nut meat processing
- **Peanuts** — pre-press crushing for maximum oil yield
