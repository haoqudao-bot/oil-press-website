## Overview

The HN300 Mini Hydraulic Oil Press is our most compact and accessible hydraulic oil press, specifically designed as a dedicated sesame oil press machine. Also known as a small sesame oil mill or traditional-style sesame oil press, it is lightweight, easy to operate, and perfectly suited for sesame and small seed oil production. With 300 tons of pressing force and a 63 Mpa system pressure, the HN300 delivers reliable oil extraction in a space-saving footprint.

The HN300 cylinder paired with a 390 mm inner diameter barrel achieves a cake surface pressure of 25 Mpa/cm², providing sufficient pressure for sesame, peanut, rapeseed, and tea seed to be fully pressed in a single cycle. Its compact 1100×1100×2350 mm overall dimensions make it ideal for workshops with limited space, while the lightweight 1.2-ton single-barrel configuration allows for easy installation and relocation.
