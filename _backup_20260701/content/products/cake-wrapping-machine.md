## Overview

The HN-CW01 Cake Wrapping Machine is an essential auxiliary equipment for hydraulic oil press operations, designed to form uniform oil seed cakes before pressing. Properly wrapped cakes ensure even pressure distribution during hydraulic pressing, which directly translates to higher oil yield and more consistent extraction results. Available in manual and semi-automatic configurations, this machine processes 60-80 cakes per hour and is compatible with tea seed cake, rapeseed cake, and all other hydraulic press cake types.

Cake wrapping is a critical preprocessing step that many operators overlook. Without properly formed cakes, hydraulic oil presses cannot achieve optimal pressure distribution, leading to uneven extraction, lower oil yield, and wasted raw material. The HN-CW01 ensures every cake is uniformly shaped and properly wrapped, allowing your hydraulic oil press to operate at peak efficiency.
