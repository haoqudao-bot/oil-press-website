## Overview

The 370 Type Industrial Seed Crusher is the upgraded, high-capacity version of our proven 220 Type — delivering 2.6× the processing throughput for large-scale oil seed crushing operations. With a powerful 18.5 KW motor and 3260 RPM rotation speed, this industrial-grade crusher handles the same hard-shell materials as the 220 Type but at significantly higher volume, making it the preferred choice for commercial and industrial oil pressing facilities.

Whether you are scaling up from the 220 Type or starting at industrial capacity, the 370 Type provides the crushing power and throughput your production line demands.

- **2.6× capacity upgrade** — processes up to 2000 kg/h, dramatically reducing batch processing time
- **High-speed 3260 RPM rotation** — delivers faster, more thorough crushing for large-volume operations
- **Same hard-shell capability** — crushes tea seeds, yellowhorn, avocado seeds and other tough materials with shells intact
- **Industrial-grade motor** — 18.5 KW power ensures consistent performance under continuous heavy load
- **Seamless scale-up** — drop-in upgrade from 220 Type with familiar operation and maintenance procedures
- **Built for continuous operation** — rugged construction designed for 24/7 industrial environments

## Applications & Suitable Materials

The 370 Type handles all the same hard-shell materials as the 220 Type, at industrial scale:

- **Tea seeds (camellia seeds)** — high-volume shell-on crushing
- **Yellowhorn (Xanthoceras sorbifolium)** — hard-shell batch processing
- **Avocado seeds** — dense pit material at scale
- **Other hard-shell seeds** — walnuts, palm kernels, and similar tough materials

Ideal for large-scale oil pressing plants, cooperative processing facilities, and any operation requiring high-throughput seed preprocessing.
