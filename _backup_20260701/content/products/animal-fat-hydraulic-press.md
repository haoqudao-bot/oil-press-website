## Overview

The HN-300AF Animal Fat Hydraulic Press is a specialized hydraulic press designed for animal fat pressing and chemical material dehydration. Featuring a large 1250 mm diameter barrel with 1100 mm height, it provides the substantial capacity needed for processing animal fats and bulky materials that standard oil presses cannot handle. With 300 tons of pressing force and a 63 Mpa system pressure, the HN-300AF efficiently extracts fat from beef tallow, chicken fat, duck fat, and pork lard, while also serving as a powerful dehydration press for chemical raw materials, white clay, oil residue, and sewage sludge.

The oversized barrel design is the key differentiator of this machine. While standard hydraulic oil presses use 390 mm barrels, the HN-300AF's 1250 mm barrel accommodates the large-volume, low-density nature of animal fat raw materials. Every barrel dimension can be customized to meet specific customer requirements, making this a truly versatile solution for non-standard pressing applications.
