## Overview

The Thermal Oil Roasting Pan is a professional-grade seed roasting system that uses thermal oil electric heating for exceptionally uniform temperature distribution and energy-efficient operation. Featuring a double-layer reinforced pot bottom, integrated motor-reducer drive, and rock wool insulation wrapped in stainless steel, this roaster delivers consistent, high-quality roasting results while minimizing energy waste.

Thermal oil heating provides a key advantage over direct-heat methods: more even heat distribution across the entire pot surface and slower cooling when heating stops, which translates to significant energy savings over long production runs. An optional electric control box adds automated heating start/stop, rotation control, and alarm functions for hands-off operation.

## Key Advantages

- **Thermal oil electric heating** — uniform heat distribution with slow cooling for energy-efficient roasting
- **Double-layer reinforced pot bottom** — enhanced durability and heat transfer efficiency
- **Integrated motor-reducer design** — compact drive system with smooth, reliable rotation
- **Rock wool insulation layer** — minimizes heat loss, reduces energy consumption
- **Stainless steel exterior** — attractive appearance, corrosion-resistant, easy to clean
- **Optional electric control box** — automated heating/rotation start-stop with alarm functions for unattended operation
- **100–150 kg per batch** — ideal capacity for small-to-medium oil pressing operations

## Applications & Suitable Materials

This roasting pan is designed for pre-press seed conditioning across a wide range of oil-bearing materials:

- **Tea seeds** — uniform roasting for optimal pressing moisture and temperature
- **Rapeseed** — consistent heat treatment for maximum oil yield
- **Peanuts** — controlled roasting to enhance flavor and pressing efficiency
- **Sesame** — gentle, even heating preserves oil quality
- **Sunflower seeds** — thorough roasting for dehulled or whole seed pressing
