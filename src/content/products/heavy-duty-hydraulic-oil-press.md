  - question: What is the oil yield of the HN355 hydraulic oil press?
    answer: The oil yield depends on the raw material. For example, sesame yields 42-54%, peanuts 40-50%, and rapeseed 35-45%. The HN355's 360-ton pressing force and bar-type slotted barrel design achieve approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary) than traditional drilled barrel presses.
  - question: What raw materials can the HN355 press?
    answer: The HN355 can press sesame, peanut, rapeseed, sunflower seed, flaxseed, tea seed, soybean, cottonseed, and more. For hard-shell materials like walnuts and almonds, we recommend our cold press or ultra-high pressure models.
  - question: How long does one pressing cycle take?
    answer: Hot pressing (with pre-roasting) completes in 30-40 minutes per batch. Cold pressing takes approximately 2 hours to preserve oil quality at low temperatures. The actual cycle time depends on factors like seed moisture content, desired cake dryness, and pressing temperature. The microcomputer control system automates the entire process from feeding to cake discharge.
  - question: What is the recommended raw material moisture content for pressing?
    answer: The optimal moisture content varies by material type and pressing method. For hot pressing, we recommend 5-8% moisture before roasting, with the roasted material reaching 3-5% moisture. For cold pressing, the ideal range is 7-9% moisture. Proper moisture control is critical — too high causes oil emulsification and yield loss, while too low increases friction and reduces throughput.
  - question: What pre-processing equipment is needed before the hydraulic press?
    answer: Complete oil production typically requires several pre-processing stages including seed cleaning and sorting to remove impurities and damaged seeds, dehulling for high-shell-content materials like peanuts with shell removal rate at least 95 percent, moisture conditioning to reach optimal levels, roasting for hot pressing at 160-180 degrees Celsius to develop flavor and color, and seed crushing for improved oil flow. The press itself is the core, but pre-processing directly impacts oil yield and quality.
  - question: What is the difference between single and double barrel configurations?
    answer: A single barrel press processes one batch at a time. A double barrel press has two barrels on sliding rails — while one barrel is pressing, you can load the other, nearly doubling your throughput. We recommend the double barrel for commercial and industrial operations.

## Overview

The HN355 Heavy Duty Hydraulic Oil Press is built for operators who demand more pressing power and greater durability. Featuring a robust 3 cm thick steel plate frame, this model delivers a 360-ton pressing force with a cake surface pressure of 30.15 Mpa/cm² — a meaningful step up from the standard HN325. Conventional oil seeds are fully pressed in a single cycle, maximizing oil yield and minimizing waste.

The reinforced steel frame not only withstands higher operating pressures but also ensures long-term structural stability under continuous heavy-duty use. If your production demands exceed what the standard model can deliver, the HN355 provides the extra margin of power you need without stepping up to a cold-press specialist.

## Key Advantages

- **Heavy-Duty Steel Frame** — 3 cm thick steel plate construction provides superior rigidity and extended service life under demanding conditions.
- **Higher Cake Pressure** — 30.15 Mpa/cm² cake surface pressure ensures conventional materials are fully pressed in a single cycle, leaving less residual oil in the cake.
- **360-Ton Pressing Force** — 10% more force than the HN325, giving you the headroom to handle tougher materials with confidence.
- **Dual-Barrel Productivity** — Double-barrel configuration with dual sliding rails lets you load one barrel while the other is pressing, significantly boosting daily output.
- **Single-Cycle Extraction** — Most conventional materials achieve complete oil extraction in one pressing cycle, reducing processing time and energy costs.

- **Bar-Type Slotted Barrel** — Haonuo's proprietary slotted barrel design provides significantly more oil extraction area than traditional drilled barrels. Oil flows faster and clearer, with approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary). No clogging, no cleaning between batches.
- **German Hydraulic System** — Ultra-high pressure combined hydraulic pump station with high-low dual pump. Working pressure up to 63 Mpa with rapid cylinder return.
- **4-Hook Cake Removal** — Innovative 4-hook mechanism ensures easy, reliable cake discharge — even after overnight pressing.
- **High Carbon Steel Frame** — High-frequency quenched and heat-treated steel for long-lasting service. Actual service life depends on operating conditions and maintenance under 360-ton pressing force.
- **Microcomputer Control** — Automated pressing cycle from loading to finished oil in minutes.
- **Energy Efficient** — Designed to reduce power consumption compared to previous-generation presses (actual savings vary by operating conditions).
- **Labor Efficient** — Only 1-2 operators needed, reducing labor requirements compared to manual pressing methods.
