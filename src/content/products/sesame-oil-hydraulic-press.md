  - question: What is the oil yield of the Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press?
    answer: The oil yield for sesame is 42-54% depending on the variety and moisture content. The narrower 300 mm barrel concentrates pressing force to 47–51 Mpa/cm² cake surface pressure, achieving complete sesame oil extraction in a single fast cycle with approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary) than traditional drilled barrel presses.
  - question: What raw materials can the Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press press?
    answer: The Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press is specifically optimized for sesame oil extraction. It also excels at pressing in-shell walnuts (except iron walnuts) in approximately 30 minutes per batch, and handles tea seed, rapeseed, peanut, soybean, lacquer oil seed, and flaxseed with even lower residual oil rates than standard models.
  - question: How long does one pressing cycle take?
    answer: A typical hot pressing cycle takes 30-40 minutes for sesame depending on the desired cake dryness and moisture content. The rapid cycle is critical for sesame's temperature-sensitive pressing — minimizing heat loss preserves oil quality and maximizes yield. For cold pressing, the cycle takes approximately 2 hours. The microcomputer control system automates the entire process from feeding to cake discharge.
  - question: What is the recommended raw material moisture content for sesame pressing?
    answer: For sesame hot pressing, we recommend 5-8% moisture content before roasting. The roasted sesame should reach 3-5% moisture for optimal oil extraction. For cold pressing, the ideal range is 7-9% moisture. Proper moisture control is critical for sesame — too high causes oil emulsification, while too low increases friction and reduces yield.
  - question: What is the difference between single and double barrel configurations?
    answer: A single barrel press processes one batch at a time. A double barrel press has two barrels on sliding rails — while one barrel is pressing, you can load the other, nearly doubling your throughput. We recommend the double barrel for commercial and industrial operations.
  - question: What power supply does the Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press require?
    answer: The Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press requires 2.2 KW motor power, compatible with both 220V/50Hz and 380V/60Hz power supplies. We can customize the electrical system to match your local power standards at no extra charge.

## Overview

The Sesame Oil Hydraulic Press is a purpose-built hydraulic oil press machine designed specifically for sesame oil extraction. Sesame pressing demands strict temperature control — to prevent heat loss and reduced oil yield, pressing must be completed in the shortest possible time. This model pairs the HN325 or HN355 cylinder with a narrower 300 mm inner diameter barrel, concentrating the pressing force to achieve 47–51 Mpa/cm² cake surface pressure, allowing sesame to be fully pressed clean in a single rapid cycle.

The 300 mm inner diameter, 800 mm high barrel holds 32.5 kg of sesame per batch. With pre-compression to create additional space, a single batch can process up to 50 kg of sesame — significantly more than Korean-style hydraulic sesame oil presses, offering superior capacity and value for money.
