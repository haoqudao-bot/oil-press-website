  - question: What is the oil yield of the HN325LB hydraulic oil press?
    answer: The oil yield depends on the raw material. The HN325LB's 325-ton pressing force and oversized 630 mm barrel with bar-type slotted design achieve approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary) than traditional drilled barrel presses. For low-pressure materials like oil sludge and residues, the large barrel capacity maximizes per-batch throughput.
  - question: What raw materials can the HN325LB press?
    answer: The HN325LB is designed for high-volume, low-pressure applications including animal oil residue, plant oil residue, oil sludge, textile sludge, fruit and vegetable dehydration, and oil field sludge. For conventional oil seeds requiring higher pressing force, we recommend our standard HN325 or cold press models.
  - question: How long does one pressing cycle take?
    answer: Hot pressing (with pre-roasting) completes in 30-40 minutes per batch. Cold pressing takes approximately 2 hours to preserve oil quality at low temperatures. The actual cycle time depends on factors like seed moisture content, desired cake dryness, and pressing temperature. The microcomputer control system automates the entire process from feeding to cake discharge.
  - question: What is the recommended raw material moisture content for pressing?
    answer: The optimal moisture content varies by material type and pressing method. For hot pressing, we recommend 5-8% moisture before roasting, with the roasted material reaching 3-5% moisture. For cold pressing, the ideal range is 7-9% moisture. Proper moisture control is critical — too high causes oil emulsification and yield loss, while too low increases friction and reduces throughput.
  - question: What is the difference between single and double barrel configurations?
    answer: A single barrel press processes one batch at a time. A double barrel press has two barrels with independent hydraulic systems — while one barrel is pressing, the top-cake cylinder on the other barrel allows simultaneous unloading and reloading, nearly doubling your throughput.

## Overview

The Large Barrel Hydraulic Oil Press is an enlarged-capacity version of the HN325 cylinder model, designed for applications that demand high single-batch processing volume rather than extreme pressing force. Equipped with two independent hydraulic systems, each barrel operates autonomously — the main cylinder presses one barrel while the top-cake cylinder on the other barrel allows simultaneous unloading and reloading. This dual-system design eliminates idle time and maximizes your daily throughput.

The 630 mm inner diameter, 1000 mm high barrel provides substantial capacity for bulk materials. With a cake surface pressure of 10.64 Mpa/cm², this model is ideal for materials that do not require high pressing force but benefit from large batch sizes, such as oil sludge, textile sludge, fruit and vegetable dehydration, and oil field waste.

## Key Advantages

- **Massive Barrel Capacity** — 630 mm inner diameter × 1000 mm height barrel handles large volumes per batch, reducing the number of cycles needed to process bulk materials.
- **Dual Independent Hydraulic Systems** — Each barrel operates independently with its own hydraulic circuit, so pressing on one side never blocks loading or unloading on the other.
- **Top-Cake Design** — The top-cake cylinder (60 Tons / 30 Mpa) pushes the cake out from the top, making unloading fast and convenient even with large-volume materials.
- **High Throughput** — Combined large barrel capacity and dual-system operation deliver the highest daily processing volume in our product line for low-pressure materials.
- **Versatile Application** — From oil residue dewatering to fruit juice extraction and industrial sludge processing, one machine serves multiple processing needs.

## Core Technology

- **Bar-Type Slotted Barrel** — Haonuo's proprietary slotted barrel design provides significantly more oil extraction area than traditional drilled barrels. Oil flows faster and clearer, with approximately 2-3% higher yield (based on internal testing, results vary). No clogging, no cleaning between batches — especially beneficial for high-volume sludge and residue processing.
- **German Hydraulic System** — Ultra-high pressure combined hydraulic pump station with high-low dual pump. Working pressure up to 60 Mpa with rapid cylinder return.
- **4-Hook Cake Removal** — Innovative 4-hook mechanism ensures easy, reliable cake discharge — even after overnight pressing of bulky materials.
- **High Carbon Steel Frame** — High-frequency quenched and heat-treated steel for long-lasting service. Actual service life depends on operating conditions and maintenance under 325-ton pressing force.
- **Microcomputer Control** — Automated pressing cycle from loading to finished product in minutes.
- **Energy Efficient** — Designed to reduce power consumption compared to previous-generation presses (actual savings vary by operating conditions).
- **Labor Efficient** — Only 1-2 operators needed, reducing labor requirements compared to manual pressing methods.
