## Overview

The Haonuo vertical screw conveyor is built entirely in stainless steel and moves oilseeds — peanut, rapeseed, soybean, sesame, sunflower seed and other granular materials — from a ground-level bin up into the hopper of a press, roaster or oil filter. At 2 metres of lift and driven by a 1.1 kW geared motor, it delivers a steady, even feed that keeps downstream machines from being starved or overfilled, and replaces the full-time worker who would otherwise be shovelling seed all day.

The 600×600×600 mm feed bin holds enough material for an unattended run, and the enclosed screw tube keeps dust and spillage inside the conveyor instead of on the workshop floor. It bolts directly onto our hydraulic oil presses, oil filter machines and roasters, so a single operator can manage feeding, pressing and filtering at the same time.

## Key Advantages

- **Steady automatic feeding** — constant screw speed gives an even flow, so roast and press consistency stay high
- **Enclosed, dust-free conveying** — material travels inside a closed tube, keeping the workshop clean and safe
- **Full stainless steel build** — food-grade, corrosion-resistant, suited to oily and humid workshops
- **600×600×600 mm bin** — holds enough for an unattended run; one operator manages feeding, pressing and filtering
- **Connects directly** — bolts onto presses, filters and roasters with no extra transfer conveyor needed
- **Low energy, long life** — 1.1 kW geared drive wastes little power; standard wearing parts keep maintenance cheap

## Structure & Build

The screw, tube, feed bin and frame are all stainless steel — clean, corrosion-resistant and food-grade. A 1.1 kW geared motor turns the screw at a steady rate, lifting material 2 metres from the ground-level bin to the hopper above. The vertical layout takes up little floor space compared with an inclined conveyor of the same lift, and the machine is easy to install and reposition.

## Applications & Suitable Materials

- **Automatic press feeding** — lifts seed into the hydraulic press hopper at the exact rate the press works best at
- **Roaster and filter feeding** — pairs with drum or pan roasters and oil filter machines
- **Granular oilseeds** — peanut, rapeseed, soybean, sesame, sunflower seed and similar free-flowing materials
- **General grain and food conveying** — suited to food-processing lines where hygiene matters
