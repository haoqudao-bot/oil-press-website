## Overview

This drum roaster is used to roast oilseeds before hot pressing, the stage that brings out aroma and lowers moisture for a higher yield. It is built as a frame, feed hopper, rotating drum and flame hood. The motor drives the drum through a V-belt and chain wheel, turning the seeds continuously, while electromagnetic heating supplies adjustable flameless heat. One-button automatic control makes the daily routine simple.

Three standard sizes are available — the 100#, the 130# and a thickened 130# — covering batches from roughly 160 kg up to 450 kg. Other sizes can be made to order to match your press throughput.

## Key Advantages

- **Even roasting, no scorching** — drum rotation plus flameless heating gives a uniform roast and protects oil flavour and colour
- **Three standard capacities** — 160–220 kg (100#), 200–350 kg (130#) or 360–450 kg (thickened 130#) per batch
- **Automatic one-button operation** — set temperature and time, the machine runs the roast and shuts off by itself
- **Flameless and safe** — electromagnetic heating removes the fire risk of gas or coal roasters and keeps the workshop cleaner
- **Sealed drum** — heats quickly and keeps heat inside for energy efficiency
- **Custom sizes available** — drum dimensions and heating power adjusted to match your production line

## Structure & Build

The enclosed drum heats quickly and keeps heat inside, and the constant turning means every seed is heated evenly — no burnt bottom layer and no half-roasted top layer, which is exactly what good pressing oil needs. Heating power and roasting temperature are set separately and held automatically, with shut-off when the set point is reached.

## Applications & Suitable Materials

- **Hot-press oil lines** — the standard pre-press roasting stage for sesame, peanut, rapeseed, sunflower, flaxseed and camellia
- **Aromatic oils** — where roast aroma is a key selling point
- **Nuts, grains and food materials** — versatile roasting beyond oilseeds
