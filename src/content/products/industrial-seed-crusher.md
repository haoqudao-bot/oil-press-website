## Overview

The 370 Type Industrial Oil Cake Crusher is designed for crushing pressed oil cakes into fine powder after oil extraction. With a powerful 18.5 KW motor and 3260 RPM rotation speed, this industrial-grade crusher efficiently processes various oil cake residues, creating value from byproducts for animal feed production or other applications.

Whether you need to recycle sesame cake, peanut cake, rapeseed cake, or other oil press residues, the 370 Type provides the crushing power and throughput your production line demands.

- **High capacity** — processes up to 2000 kg/h, dramatically reducing batch processing time
- **High-speed 3260 RPM rotation** — delivers faster, more thorough crushing for large-volume operations
- **Versatile cake processing** — crushes sesame cake, peanut cake, rapeseed cake and various oil press residues
- **Industrial-grade motor** — 18.5 KW power ensures consistent performance under continuous heavy load
- **Fine powder output** — produces uniform particle size suitable for animal feed and other applications
- **Built for continuous operation** — rugged construction designed for 24/7 industrial environments

The 370 Type specializes in processing pressed oil cake residues:

- **Sesame cake** — high-protein residue after oil extraction
- **Peanut cake** — nutrient-rich byproduct from peanut oil pressing
- **Rapeseed cake** — high-fiber residue ideal for animal feed
- **Other oil press cakes** — sunflower, cottonseed, and various oil cake residues

Ideal for oil pressing plants, feed mills, and any operation requiring efficient oil cake recycling.
