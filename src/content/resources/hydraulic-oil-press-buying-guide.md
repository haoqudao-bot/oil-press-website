## Introduction

Choosing the right hydraulic oil press is a critical investment decision that directly impacts your production capacity, oil yield, and overall profitability. With multiple models available, understanding the key differences between them is essential for making an informed choice.

## Key Factors to Consider

### 1. Raw Material Type
- Conventional seeds: sesame, peanut, rapeseed, soybean, tea seed
- Hard-shell materials: walnut, almond, apricot kernel, tiger nut
- Cold-press only: materials requiring low-temperature extraction

### 2. Production Scale
- Small-scale: 500-1,500 kg/day
- Medium-scale: 1,500-3,000 kg/day
- Large-scale: 3,000+ kg/day

### 3. Pressing Method
- Hot pressing: higher yield, faster cycle time (30-40 minutes/batch)
- Cold pressing: premium quality oil, longer cycle time (2 hours/batch)

## Selection Decision Tree

### Step 1: Determine Your Primary Material

**Conventional seeds only:**
- Small scale -> HN325
- Medium scale -> HN355
- Large scale -> HN500

**Cold-press materials:**
- Standard cold press -> HN426
- High-volume cold press -> HN480
- Maximum yield cold press -> HN500

**Mixed materials:**
- Balanced performance -> HN480
- Maximum versatility -> HN500

## Single vs Double Barrel Configuration

**Single barrel:**
- Lower upfront cost
- Simpler operation
- Ideal for small-scale or startup operations

**Double barrel:**
- Nearly doubles throughput
- Continuous operation capability
- Recommended for commercial and industrial use
