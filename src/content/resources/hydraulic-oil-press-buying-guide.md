## Introduction

A hydraulic oil press is typically the largest single purchase in a new oil mill, and it is bought once for many years of daily cycles. The tempting approach — compare tonnage ratings and prices, pick the middle option — is exactly how buyers end up with the wrong machine: a low-pressure model that cannot finish hard nuts, a single tabung press that bottlenecks a two-person crew, or a frame built from ordinary plate that flexes after a year of 50 MPa cycles.

This buying guide works through the decision in the order it should actually be made: raw material and process first, then daily volume, then pressure and tabung press configuration, then build quality, supplier evaluation, and total budget. It maps each step to the current Haonuo model range so you can translate your requirements into a concrete shortlist.

## Step 1: Define What You Will Actually Press

The seed, not the catalogue, picks the machine.

- **Conventional oil seeds** (sesame, peanut, rapeseed, sunflower, soybean, tea seed): hot pressing works well on standard 27–30 MPa cake pressure; cold pressing benefits from higher pressure.
- **Hard-shell, high-value nuts** (walnut, almond, apricot kernel): cold press only, at 42 MPa or more; a standard machine will leave these nuts oily and under-extracted.
- **Coconut / VCO**: needs a large 600 mm food-grade stainless tabung press and pre-cutting equipment.
- **Mixed campaigns**: if the plan includes even one hard-nut product, size the press for that product rather than for the easiest seed.

Write down the top three seeds, their percentage of planned volume, hot versus cold split, and expected monthly growth. Every later question references that list.

## Step 2: Size the Daily Volume

| Scale | Seed Processed per Day | Typical Configuration |
|-------|------------------------|-----------------------|
| Startup / small | 500–1,500 kg | One HN325, single tabung press |
| Commercial medium | 1,500–3,000 kg | HN365 or HN426, double tabung press |
| Industrial | 3,000+ kg | HN500 double tabung press, or multiple presses |

Be careful with quoted daily figures: hot pressing and cold pressing are different capacities on the same machine. An HN325 reaches roughly 900–1,000 kg per 8-hour shift hot (single tabung press) but 300–400 kg cold; double tabung press lift those figures to roughly 1,200–1,400 kg hot and 600–700 kg cold. Plan capacity against your actual product mix, and leave 20–30% headroom for growth and for the inevitable days when roasting or filtration run behind.

## Step 3: Understand the Three Pressure Numbers

Sellers often quote one big tonnage figure, but three numbers matter and they answer different questions:

1. **Rated pressing force (tonnes)** — total ram force; impressive on a brochure, but meaningless without the tabung press area it acts on.
2. **System pressure (MPa)** — hydraulic circuit pressure; indicates pump and cylinder class.
3. **Cake-surface pressure (MPa)** — force divided by tabung press cross-section; this is the number that actually extracts oil from hard seeds.

A smaller tabung press can deliver high cake pressure on modest force, which is why cake-surface pressure is the specification to match to your seed. Hot pressing conventional seeds needs about 27–30 MPa; cold pressing and hard nuts need 42–52 MPa.

## Step 4: Choose Single or Double Tabung Press

| Factor | Single Tabung Press | Double Tabung Press |
|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Upfront cost | Lower | Higher |
| Throughput | One batch at a time | Load/unload one station while the other presses |
| Labour rhythm | Forgiving; suits 1–2 staff | Needs a steady loading rhythm to pay off |
| Fit | Startups, niche seeds, testing campaigns | Commercial and industrial daily output |

Double tabung press share one frame and hydraulic pack, so the upgrade adds throughput rather than a second complete machine — many buyers start single and add the second station later where the design permits; confirm this upgrade path before ordering.

## Step 5: Inspect Build Quality (Before Price)

At equal tonnage, two presses can be built very differently. Ask for specifics:

- **Cylinder steel:** 27SiMn alloy steel (GB/T 3077), heat-treated and pressure-tested, is the standard for high-pressure hydraulic cylinders.
- **Tabung Press:** Q690 high-strength welded construction, nickel-plated inside and out so no bare carbon steel touches the oil; strip-slotted walls rather than drilled holes.
- **Frame:** structural steel such as Q355B; on high-tonnage frames ask about plate thickness (3 cm on the HN365) and weld inspection.
- **Hydraulics:** branded pump and valve components, cartridge-valve block, pressure relief and gauge, accessible filters.
- **Controls:** microcomputer or contactor control with emergency stop and motor protection; star-delta starting on larger frames.
- **Food contact:** 304 stainless or nickel-plated contact surfaces for edible oil; 600 mm stainless tabung press for coconut.

A machine whose seller cannot name the cylinder steel or tabung press plating is effectively asking you to trust an unknowable component under a million newton of force.

## Step 6: Evaluate the Supplier

Price comparison should happen between suppliers who pass this screen, not instead of it:

1. **Factory evidence:** workshop photos or video, casting/fabrication capability, in-house pressure testing — not only trading-company renders.
2. **Specifications in writing:** tonnage, system and cake pressure, tabung press material and diameter, motor, weight, and dimensions on the proforma invoice.
3. **Voltage built to order:** 220–240 V single phase or 380–415 V three phase, 50/60 Hz, matched to your grid.
4. **Spare parts and documentation:** parts list with part numbers, wiring and hydraulic diagrams, operation and maintenance manual in English.
5. **Warranty and response:** what is covered, for how long, and how technical questions are answered after installation.
6. **References in your region or seed type:** a running machine pressing your seed is worth more than a certificate wall.
7. **Trade terms:** FOB port, CIF to your port, or door-to-door; lead time (standard builds commonly 15–25 working days, custom tabung press 30–45 days).

## Step 7: Build the Realistic Budget

The press is the headline cost, but a working line includes more. A typical budget breakdown for a small-to-medium mill:

| Item | Approx. Share of Equipment Spend |
|------|----------------------------------|
| Hydraulic press, single/double tabung press | 35–45% |
| Cleaner, dehuller, crusher | 15–20% |
| Roaster and conditioner (hot press) | 15–25% |
| Filter press and storage tanks | 10–15% |
| Conveyors, installation, electrics | 10–15% |

Then add: freight and insurance, import duty and VAT, a spare-parts package (seals, filter elements, O-rings, gauge), commissioning and operator training, and working capital for the first seed purchases. Buyers who budget only the press FOB price routinely stall at installation for lack of cable, breakers, or a correctly sized generator — the [electrical guide](/resources/oil-press-electrical-requirements-guide/) covers these numbers.

## Decision Tree Summary

1. Hard nuts or cold-press premium products planned? → HN426 minimum; HN500 for maximum recovery.
2. Conventional seeds only, startup volume? → HN325 single tabung press.
3. Conventional seeds, all-day commercial volume? → HN365 double tabung press.
4. Mixed seeds with growth ambitions? → HN426 (flexibility) or HN500 (headroom).
5. More than one press's worth of output? → multiple presses beat one oversized machine for maintenance flexibility.

## Questions to Send to the Factory

A good quotation request is short and specific: seed list with percentages, hot/cold split, target kg per day, single or three-phase voltage, desired tabung press material, port and trade term, and whether coconut or food-grade tabung press are required. Return should be a model recommendation with cake pressure, throughput assumptions, line drawing or dimensions, lead time, spares list, and an itemised price — not a single number for "an oil press machine."

## Conclusion

The right hydraulic oil press is the smallest machine that genuinely clears three hurdles: enough cake-surface pressure for your hardest seed, enough daily capacity for your hot/cold product mix with headroom, and build quality documented down to cylinder steel and tabung press plating. Buying bigger than you need wastes capital; buying weaker than your hardest seed wastes yield on every batch.
