## Introduction

Selecting the right raw materials is critical for successful oil production. Different seeds and nuts have varying oil content, processing requirements, and market values.

## Understanding Raw Material Properties

### Key Characteristics

1. Oil Content: Higher oil content = higher yield per kg
2. Moisture Content: Critical for optimal extraction (too dry = reduced yield, too wet = emulsification)
3. Seed Size and Shape: Affects crushing efficiency
4. Hardness: Determines pressing pressure needed
5. Market Value: Premium oils = higher profit margin

## Common Oil-Bearing Materials

### 1. Sesame Seeds
- Oil Content: 45-55%
- Optimal Moisture: 5-6% hot press, 7-8% cold press
- Dehulling: 98%+ removal recommended
- Roasting: 160-180掳C for 25-30 minutes
- Yield: Hot 48-54%, Cold 42-48%
- Recommended Models: HN325, HN355, HN426

### 2. Peanuts (Groundnuts)
- Oil Content: 45-50%
- Optimal Moisture: 5-7% hot press
- Dehulling: 95%+ removal recommended
- Roasting: 165-185掳C for 30-35 minutes
- Yield: Hot 45-50%, Cold 38-42%
- Recommended Models: HN325, HN355, HN480

### 3. Rapeseed (Canola)
- Oil Content: 40-45%
- Optimal Moisture: 6-8% hot press
- Dehulling: 85-90% removal
- Roasting: 150-170掳C for 20-25 minutes
- Yield: Hot 38-42%, Cold 32-36%
- Recommended Models: HN325, HN355, HN500

### 4. Walnuts
- Oil Content: 60-70%
- Optimal Moisture: 7-9% cold press
- Dehulling: Remove shells completely
- No roasting (cold press only)
- Yield: Cold 45-50%
- Recommended Models: HN426, HN480, HN500

### 5. Almonds
- Oil Content: 50-60%
- Optimal Moisture: 7-9% cold press
- Dehulling: Remove shells completely
- No roasting (cold press only)
- Yield: Cold 42-48%
- Recommended Models: HN426, HN480, HN500

### 6. Coconut
- Oil Content: 60-70%
- Optimal Moisture: 6-8%
- Dehulling: Remove outer shell and husk
- Grating: 1-2 mm particles
- Yield: Hot 60-65%, Cold (VCO) 55-60%
- Recommended Models: HN426, HN480, HN500

### 7. Soybeans
- Oil Content: 18-22%
- Optimal Moisture: 7-9% hot press
- Dehulling: 90%+ removal
- Roasting: 140-160掳C for 25-30 minutes
- Yield: Hot 18-22%, Cold 15-18%
- Recommended Models: HN480, HN500

### 8. Tea Seeds
- Oil Content: 30-40%
- Optimal Moisture: 5-7% hot press
- Dehulling: Remove shells
- Roasting: 150-170掳C for 25-30 minutes
- Yield: Hot 28-35%
- Recommended Models: HN325, HN355, HN426

## Material Preparation Best Practices

### Seed Cleaning
- Remove impurities (stones, metal, dirt)
- Separate damaged and broken seeds
- Target: < 0.5% impurities

### Moisture Conditioning
- Too dry: Add moisture with spray humidifier
- Too wet: Dry with hot air dryer or sun drying

### Roasting (Hot Pressing Only)
- Sesame: 160-180掳C, 25-30 min
- Peanut: 165-185掳C, 30-35 min
- Rapeseed: 150-170掳C, 20-25 min

### Crushing
- Optimal particle size: 2-4 mm for most materials
- 3-5 mm for nuts

## Storage and Handling

### Storage Conditions
- Temperature: 15-25掳C
- Humidity: < 65% relative humidity
- Duration: < 6 months for best quality

### Pest Control
- Use rodent-proof containers
- Regular inspection
- Appropriate pest control measures
