## Introduction

Peanut oil is one of the most popular cooking oils globally, prized for its high smoke point, neutral flavor, and nutritional benefits. This comprehensive guide covers every aspect of peanut oil production, from raw material selection through pressing to finished oil.

## Raw Material Preparation

### Understanding Peanut Types

| Peanut Type | Characteristics | Best For |
|-------------|----------------|----------|
| Runner | Medium-sized, uniform kernels | General oil production |
| Virginia | Large kernels, high quality | Premium oil, export |
| Spanish | Small kernels, high oil content | High yield production |
| Valencia | Multiple kernels per pod | Specialty markets |

### Raw Material Requirements

1. **Moisture Content**: 7-9% for cold pressing, 5-7% for hot pressing
2. **Oil Content**: 45-55% (good quality peanuts)
3. **Impurity Level**: Less than 0.5%
4. **Aflatoxin Level**: AFB1 ≤ 20 µg/kg (retail compliance)

### Processing Steps

**Step 1: Shelling**
- Required for all peanut oil production
- Target: 95%+ shell removal rate
- Equipment: Disc dehuller or hammer mill

**Step 2: Cleaning**
- Remove stones, metal, and other hard impurities
- Magnetic separation for metal contaminants
- Gravity separation for lighter impurities

**Step 3: Sorting**
- Visual inspection for moldy or damaged kernels
- Optional: Optical sorting for color and quality

## Hot Pressing Process

### Hot Pressing Overview

Hot pressing is the most common method for peanut oil production, offering higher yields and faster processing times.

### Equipment Configuration

**Complete Hot Press Line:**
1. 5 x Electric Heating Flat Pans (100 kg/batch each)
2. 5 x HN325 Hydraulic Oil Press (100 kg/batch each)
3. Pneumatic Multi-layer Filter Press
4. Settling Tanks
5. Stainless Steel Storage Tanks

### Production Parameters

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Pre-pressing Rate | ~500 kg/h |
| Roasting Temperature | 160-180°C |
| Roasting Time | 25-35 minutes |
| Pressing Cycle | 30-40 minutes/batch |
| Total Cycle (2 barrels) | ~1.5 hours |
| System Pressure | 60 MPa |
| Motor Power | 2.2 kW |

### Expected Yields

| Material Condition | Oil Yield | Residual Oil |
|-------------------|-----------|--------------|
| Good quality kernels | 45-55% | 6-8% |
| Average quality kernels | 40-45% | 8-10% |

## Cold Pressing Process

### Cold Pressing Overview

Cold pressing preserves the natural flavor and nutritional properties of peanut oil, producing a premium product with higher market value.

### Equipment Configuration

**Cold Press Line:**
1. HN355, HN426, HN480, or HN500 Hydraulic Oil Press
2. Precision Temperature Control System
3. Pneumatic Multi-layer Filter Press
4. Temperature-Controlled Storage Tanks

### Production Parameters

| Model | Pressing Force | Cycle Time | Best For |
|-------|---------------|------------|----------|
| HN355 | 370 tons | ~2 hours/batch | Entry-level cold press |
| HN426 | 500 tons | ~2 hours/batch | Standard cold press |
| HN480 | 560 tons | ~2 hours/batch | High-volume cold press |
| HN500 | 620 tons | ~2 hours/batch | Premium cold press |

### Cold Press Advantages

- Preserves natural peanut aroma
- Retains vitamin E and resveratrol
- Product premium: 2-3 times higher market value
- No chemical processing

## Quality Control

### Aflatoxin Management

Aflatoxin contamination is a critical concern in peanut oil production. Implement a comprehensive control system:

**Control Points:**

| Stage | Control Measure | Acceptance Standard |
|-------|----------------|---------------------|
| Raw Material | Batch inspection | AFB1 ≤ 20 µg/kg |
| Sorting | Optical color sorting | Remove moldy kernels |
| Roasting | Temperature monitoring | 160-180°C |
| Filtration | Activated carbon adsorption | AFB1 ≤ 20 µg/kg |
| Finished Oil | Batch testing | AFB1 ≤ 20 µg/kg (GB 2761) |

### Oil Quality Standards

| Parameter | Standard |
|-----------|----------|
| Acid Value | ≤ 1.0 mg KOH/g |
| Peroxide Value | ≤ 10 mmol/kg |
| Moisture | ≤ 0.1% |
| Impurities | ≤ 0.05% |

## Post-Pressing Processing

### Filtration System

**Multi-stage Filtration:**
1. Primary Filtration: 100-200 mesh
2. Fine Filtration: 200-300 mesh
3. Polishing: 300-500 mesh (optional for premium oil)

**Filtration Methods:**
- Plate filter press
- Bag filter
- Centrifugal filter
- Pneumatic multi-layer filter

### Oil Settling and Storage

**Settling Process:**
1. Allow oil to settle 24-48 hours
2. Particles sink to bottom
3. Decant clear oil from top

**Storage Requirements:**
- Stainless steel tanks
- Temperature ≤ 25°C
- Protected from light
- Proper ventilation

## By-Product Utilization

### Peanut Cake

Peanut cake is a valuable by-product with multiple uses:

| Application | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Animal Feed | High protein content (45-50%) |
| Fertilizer | Organic nitrogen source |
| Human Consumption | After further processing |
| Industrial | Extraction of other components |

### Cake Handling Options

1. Direct collection and bagging
2. Temporary storage bins
3. Integrated crushing for feed
4. Pelletizing for easy handling

## Production Line Layout Considerations

### Space Requirements

| Scale | Area | Height |
|-------|------|--------|
| Small | 50-80 m² | 3 m |
| Medium | 100-150 m² | 3-4 m |
| Large | 200-300 m² | 4-5 m |

### Workflow Design

**Optimal Flow:**
1. Raw material receiving →
2. Shelling →
3. Cleaning →
4. Roasting (hot press) →
5. Pressing →
6. Filtration →
7. Settling →
8. Storage →
9. Packaging

### Equipment Placement

- Presses in central location
- Filtration near presses for efficient transfer
- Storage tanks away from direct sunlight
- Easy access for maintenance and cleaning
