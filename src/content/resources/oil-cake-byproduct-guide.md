## Introduction

Oil cake is the solid residue remaining after oil extraction from oilseeds. This valuable by-product represents approximately 50-60% of the original seed weight and contains high levels of protein, fiber, and other nutrients. Proper utilization of oil cake can significantly improve the overall profitability of oil production operations.

## Understanding Oil Cake Composition

### Nutritional Analysis

| Component | Peanut Cake | Sesame Cake | Rapeseed Cake | Soybean Cake |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|---------------|--------------|
| Protein | 45-50% | 35-40% | 38-42% | 40-45% |
| Fat | 6-10% | 8-12% | 3-6% | 1-3% |
| Fiber | 8-12% | 6-8% | 10-15% | 5-8% |
| Carbohydrates | 20-25% | 25-30% | 25-30% | 25-30% |
| Ash | 5-7% | 5-7% | 6-8% | 5-7% |

### Key Nutrients

1. **Protein**: High-quality plant protein for animal feed
2. **Amino Acids**: Contains essential amino acids like lysine, methionine
3. **Minerals**: Rich in phosphorus, calcium, magnesium, potassium
4. **Vitamins**: B vitamins, vitamin E

## Animal Feed Applications

### Poultry Feed

**Benefits:**
- High protein promotes growth and egg production
- Good source of energy
- Cost-effective alternative to soybean meal

**Recommended Inclusion:**
- Broilers: 10-20% of diet
- Layers: 8-15% of diet
- Turkeys: 10-18% of diet

### Livestock Feed

**Benefits:**
- Improves milk production in dairy cattle
- Enhances weight gain in beef cattle
- Suitable for sheep and goats

**Recommended Inclusion:**
- Dairy cattle: 15-25% of concentrate mix
- Beef cattle: 10-20% of diet
- Sheep/goats: 10-15% of diet

### Aquaculture Feed

**Benefits:**
- High protein for fish growth
- Sustainable alternative to fish meal
- Good digestibility

**Recommended Inclusion:**
- Tilapia: 15-25%
- Catfish: 10-20%
- Shrimp: 10-15%

### Swine Feed

**Benefits:**
- Promotes weight gain
- Improves feed conversion ratio
- Cost-effective

**Recommended Inclusion:**
- Growing pigs: 10-20%
- Finishing pigs: 8-15%
- Sows: 10-15%

## Human Food Applications

### Traditional Uses

**Peanut Cake (Kuli-Kuli):**
- Popular snack in West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana)
- Deep-fried cake balls
- High protein, energy-dense

**Tempeh Production:**
- Fermented soybean cake
- Traditional Indonesian food
- High nutritional value

### Modern Applications

**Protein Isolate:**
- Extracted protein for food products
- Used in vegetarian meat substitutes
- Protein supplements

**Gluten-Free Products:**
- Oil cake flour for gluten-free baking
- High fiber content
- Nutrient-rich

## Agricultural Applications

### Organic Fertilizer

**Benefits:**
- Slow-release nitrogen source
- Improves soil structure
- Enhances soil microbial activity

**Application Methods:**
- Direct application: 500-1,000 kg/ha
- Composting: Mix with organic waste
- Liquid fertilizer: Extract nutrients

### Soil Amendment

**Benefits:**
- Improves water retention
- Enhances soil aeration
- Increases cation exchange capacity

### Animal Bedding

**Benefits:**
- Absorbent material
- Cost-effective
- Can be composted after use

## Industrial Applications

### Biogas Production

**Process:**
1. Mix oil cake with water
2. Anaerobic digestion
3. Produce biogas (methane)
4. Use for energy production

**Yield:**
- 200-300 m³ biogas per ton of oil cake
- 50-60% methane content

### Biofuel Production

**Process:**
1. Extract residual oil
2. Convert to biodiesel
3. Use as transportation fuel

### Activated Carbon Production

**Process:**
1. Carbonize oil cake
2. Activate with steam or chemicals
3. Use for filtration and purification

## Processing Methods

### Crushing and Grinding

**Equipment:**
- Hammer mill
- Roller mill
- Disk mill

**Particle Size:**
- Animal feed: 1-3 mm
- Fertilizer: 2-5 mm
- Industrial: Fine powder

### Pelletizing

**Benefits:**
- Improved handling
- Reduced dust
- Higher bulk density

**Equipment:**
- Flat die pellet mill
- Ring die pellet mill

**Pellet Size:**
- 4-8 mm diameter

### Extraction of Value-Added Products

**1. Residual Oil Extraction:**
- Solvent extraction
- Supercritical CO2 extraction
- Additional 2-3% oil recovery

**2. Protein Extraction:**
- Alkaline extraction
- Acid precipitation
- Protein isolate production

**3. Phytochemical Extraction:**
- Antioxidants
- Phytosterols
- Saponins

## Quality Control

### Testing Parameters

| Parameter | Acceptance Standard | Test Method |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|
| Protein Content | > 40% | Kjeldahl method |
| Moisture | < 12% | Oven drying |
| Ash | < 8% | Combustion |
| Aflatoxin | < 20 µg/kg | HPLC |
| Free Fatty Acids | < 2% | Titration |

### Storage Requirements

- Dry, well-ventilated storage
- Temperature < 25°C
- Relative humidity < 65%
- Use moisture-resistant bags
