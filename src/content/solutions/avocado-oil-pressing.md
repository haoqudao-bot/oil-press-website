## Overview

Avocado oil has surged in popularity as a premium cooking and cosmetic oil, prized for its high smoke point, rich monounsaturated fat content, and distinctive buttery flavor. A specialized **avocado oil press machine** is essential for producers who want to extract this high-value oil while preserving the nutrients and natural green color that justify premium pricing.

Haonuo's avocado oil pressing solution is built around our cold press hydraulic technology, which is the ideal method for avocado oil extraction. Unlike screw presses that generate friction heat up to 120°C, our hydraulic **avocado oil extraction machine** operates below 60°C, preserving the oleic acid, vitamin E, lutein, and chlorophyll that give avocado oil its health benefits and characteristic emerald green color.

This solution is designed for the growing avocado oil market in Mexico, Kenya, New Zealand, South Africa, and the United States, where demand for premium cold-pressed avocado oil continues to outpace supply.

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Variety**: Hass avocados have the highest oil content (up to 25% when fully ripe)
- **Ripeness**: Fully ripe avocados yield significantly more oil than underripe fruit
- **Drying**: Dehydrating avocado pulp before pressing can double the yield percentage
- **Pressing method**: Cold pressing preserves quality but yields slightly less than hot pressing

## Recommended Process

1. **Fruit Selection** — Use fully ripe avocados with no bruising or rot. Hass variety is preferred for its high oil content. Overripe fruit can still be pressed but may produce darker oil.
2. **Pitting & Peeling** — Remove pits and skins. Only the flesh (pulp) is used for premium avocado oil production.
3. **Mashing** — Mash avocado flesh into a smooth paste using the High Speed Seed Crusher. Uniform paste ensures consistent pressing.
4. **Drying (Recommended)** — Dehydrate avocado paste to reduce moisture content from approximately 70% to below 15%. This dramatically improves oil yield and reduces emulsion issues.
5. **Cold Pressing** — Feed dried avocado paste into the Cold Press Hydraulic Oil Press (HN426). The low-temperature pressing preserves chlorophyll and nutrients. Each batch takes 30-40 minutes (hot press) or approximately 2 hours (cold press).
6. **Filtration** — Pass crude avocado oil through the Pneumatic Oil Filter Press for clear, premium oil. Multiple filtration passes may be needed for the clearest result.
7. **Settling** — Allow filtered oil to settle for 48-72 hours. Any remaining water will separate from the oil during this period.
8. **Packaging** — Bottle avocado oil in dark glass bottles to protect from light oxidation. Premium avocado oil should be stored at cool temperatures.

**Can I press avocado oil from fresh (undried) avocado?**
Yes, but with lower yield. Fresh avocado pulp contains about 70% water, which creates emulsions and reduces oil yield. Drying the pulp before pressing can double your effective yield. For small-scale production, fresh pressing is feasible; for commercial operations, drying is strongly recommended.

**What makes avocado oil so expensive?**
Avocado oil commands premium prices because of the relatively low oil content of fresh avocados (15-25%), the labor-intensive preparation required, and the growing global demand for this health-conscious oil. Cold-pressed avocado oil sells at a significant premium in retail markets.

**Can I press avocado seeds for oil?**
Yes, avocado seeds contain 3-5% oil and can be pressed using our equipment. However, the yield is very low and the oil has different properties than pulp oil. Most producers focus on pulp oil for the premium market.
