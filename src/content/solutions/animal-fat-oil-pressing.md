## Overview

Lard and tallow are among the oldest traded fats in the world — and demand is growing again. Lard sells into bakery, frying and traditional food markets in Mexico, China, Spain and Central-Eastern Europe, while tallow feeds soap, feed, oleochemical and biodiesel industries in Pakistan, India, the Middle East and Egypt. For slaughterhouses, meat processors and rendering plants, fat trimmings are a low-cost byproduct; a dependable **animal fat press machine** turns that byproduct into a sellable fat business.

The role of the hydraulic press is specific and honest: heat rendering melts out most of the fat, but the remaining cracklings (greaves) still hold valuable residual fat. Pressing the hot cracklings with a hydraulic **lard and tallow press machine** recovers that residue, raising your total fat recovery by several points and producing a pressed crackling cake that sells as feed or pet-food ingredient.

Low-temperature rendering plus hydraulic pressing also protects quality: gentle heat keeps the fat light in color and mild in flavor for food-grade lard, while harder beef and mutton tallow is pressed hot for maximum recovery into industrial and halal food markets.
