## Overview

Shea butter is a vital commodity for West African economies and a highly valued ingredient in the global cosmetics and food industries. A robust **shea butter press machine** is essential for producers who want to move beyond traditional hand-extraction methods and scale their operations to meet growing international demand.

Haonuo's shea butter pressing solution addresses the unique challenges of shea nut processing — the hard nut shell, the high fat content, and the need for thorough extraction. Our high-pressure hydraulic **shea butter extraction machine** applies the sustained pressure needed to extract maximum butter from shea nut paste, achieving residual fat rates below 5% that traditional methods simply cannot match.

This solution is designed specifically for West African operating conditions, with robust construction, simple maintenance requirements, and the ability to handle the full range of shea nut varieties found across the Sahel region.

## Oil Yield Expectations

| Raw Material | Fat Content | Expected Press Yield | Residual Fat Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Shea Nuts (Whole) | 45-55% | 38-48% | ≤ 6% |

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Nut quality**: Fresh, well-dried shea nuts yield significantly more butter than old or moldy nuts
- **Roasting**: Proper roasting at 140-160°C breaks down cell walls and increases yield by 5-8%
- **Grinding**: Fine grinding into paste before pressing dramatically improves extraction efficiency
- **Pressing pressure**: Shea butter requires higher pressure (40-60 MPa) than most other oils for complete extraction

## Recommended Process

1. **Nut Collection & Sorting** — Collect mature shea nuts and sort to remove damaged, moldy, or immature nuts. Quality nuts are essential for premium shea butter.
2. **De-shelling** — Crack shea nut shells to extract the kernels. This can be done manually or with our seed crusher for higher throughput.
3. **Washing & Drying** — Wash kernels to remove shell fragments, then sun-dry or oven-dry to moisture content below 7%.
4. **Roasting** — Roast dried kernels at 140-160°C for 20-30 minutes using the Thermal Oil Roasting Pan. This develops the characteristic shea butter aroma and increases yield.
5. **Grinding** — Grind roasted kernels into a smooth paste using the seed crusher. Fine paste is critical for efficient pressing.
6. **Hydraulic Pressing** — Feed shea paste into the Heavy Duty or Ultra High Pressure Hydraulic Oil Press. Apply sustained high pressure for 40-50 minutes per batch.
7. **Filtration** — Pass crude shea butter through the Pneumatic Oil Filter Press for pure, unrefined shea butter.
8. **Cooling & Packaging** — Allow filtered shea butter to cool and solidify naturally. Package in blocks or containers for distribution.

**What is the difference between unrefined and refined shea butter?**
Unrefined (raw) shea butter retains its natural ivory color, nutty aroma, and all vitamins and nutrients. It commands the highest price in the cosmetics market. Refined shea butter is processed to remove color and odor, making it suitable for food industry applications and white cosmetic formulations.
