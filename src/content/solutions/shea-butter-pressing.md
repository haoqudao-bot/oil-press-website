**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Nut quality**: Fresh, well-dried shea nuts yield significantly more butter than old or moldy nuts
- **Roasting**: Proper roasting at 140-160°C breaks down cell walls and increases yield by 5-8%
- **Grinding**: Fine grinding into paste before pressing dramatically improves extraction efficiency
- **Pressing pressure**: Shea butter requires higher pressure (40-60 MPa) than most other oils for complete extraction
