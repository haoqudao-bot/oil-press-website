  - question: Why does rice bran need to be pressed fresh?
    answer: Rice bran contains active lipase enzymes that break oil down into free fatty acids within hours of milling. Bran pressed fresh (within 24-48 hours) or heat-stabilized immediately after milling gives edible-grade oil; stale bran produces high-FFA oil only fit for industrial use. We give you this honest guidance because bran freshness, not the machine, decides your oil grade.
  - question: What is the oil yield from rice bran?
    answer: Rice bran typically contains 15-23% oil. A hydraulic press recovers roughly 9-15% of bran weight as oil depending on bran freshness, stabilization and conditioning. Solvent extraction plants recover more, but at far higher investment. Results vary with bran freshness — press fresh or stabilized bran for the best yield.
  - question: Can I produce cold-pressed rice bran oil?
    answer: Yes. Cold pressing stabilized bran below 60°C produces premium cold-pressed rice bran oil that retains gamma-oryzanol and natural antioxidants. Yield is slightly lower than hot pressing, but cold-pressed rice bran oil commands premium prices in health-food markets.

## Rice Bran Oil Press Overview

Rice bran oil is one of the healthiest cooking oils in the world — rich in gamma-oryzanol, vitamin E and natural antioxidants — and a fast-growing premium oil in India, Bangladesh, Japan and Southeast Asia. For rice millers, bran is a low-cost byproduct that is often sold cheap or discarded; a dependable **rice bran oil press machine** turns that byproduct into a high-value oil business.

The challenge with rice bran is not the pressing — it is the clock. Freshly milled bran contains active lipase enzymes that raise free fatty acids within hours, ruining the oil grade. Haonuo's rice bran pressing solution is built around this reality: stabilize the bran with heat (our roaster or steamer) within hours of milling, then press with a hydraulic **rice bran oil extraction machine** for clean, low-FFA oil.

The solution supports both cold pressing (for premium cold-pressed rice bran oil that retains gamma-oryzanol) and hot pressing with steaming (for higher yield), giving you the versatility to serve health-food and mass-market segments with a single production line.

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Bran freshness**: The single biggest factor. Press within 24-48 hours of milling or heat-stabilize immediately — free fatty acids rise within hours
- **Stabilization**: Steaming or roasting the bran stops lipase activity and increases press yield
- **Moisture content**: Optimal pressing moisture is 6-9%
- **Pressing method**: Cold pressing yields slightly less but produces premium oil for health-food markets
