## Recommended Process

1. **Seed Receiving & Storage** — Set up proper storage for bulk seed deliveries. Maintain seed quality with dry, ventilated storage conditions.
2. **Automated Seed Processing** — Use the Integrated Feeder Crusher Roaster for automated cleaning, crushing, and roasting in a single pass. This reduces labor and ensures consistent preparation.
3. **Hydraulic Pressing** — Feed prepared seeds into the Heavy Duty (HN355) or Cold Press (HN426) with double-barrel configuration. Each barrel processes independently, enabling near-continuous operation.
4. **Filtration** — Pass crude oil through the Pneumatic Oil Filter Press for commercial-grade clarity. High-volume filtration matches press throughput.
5. **Quality Control** — Test oil for acidity, clarity, and sensory characteristics. Implement batch tracking for commercial traceability.
6. **Packaging** — Package finished oil in bottles, jerry cans, or drums for wholesale and retail distribution. Commercial operations typically use semi-automated filling equipment.

**What is the difference between single and double barrel?**
A double-barrel configuration has two independent pressing chambers that operate simultaneously, effectively doubling your throughput. Both barrels can process the same seed type or different types at the same time. The footprint increase is minimal compared to the production gain.

## Single vs. Double Barrel Comparison

| Feature | Single Barrel | Double Barrel |
|---|---|---|
| Daily Capacity | 1,000-2,500 kg | 2,000-5,000 kg |
| Barrels | 1 pressing chamber | 2 independent chambers |
| Operation | Batch-by-batch | Near-continuous (alternate barrels) |
| Floor Space | ~3 m² | ~4.5 m² |
| Power Consumption | Single motor | Dual motor (shared hydraulics) |
| Labor Required | 1 operator | 1-2 operators |
| Best For | Startups, single-seed production | Multi-seed, high-volume operations |
| Price Premium | Base price | ~60-70% more than single |

**When to choose single barrel:** You're starting out, processing one seed type, or have limited space and budget.

**When to choose double barrel:** You need higher daily throughput, want to process two seed types simultaneously, or need near-continuous operation to meet commercial orders.
