## Flaxseed Oil Pressing Solution

Flaxseed (linseed) oil is one of the richest plant-based sources of omega-3 fatty acids, making it a premium product in the health supplement and gourmet cooking markets. Cold pressing is essential for flaxseed oil to preserve its delicate alpha-linolenic acid (ALA) content.

### Why Cold Press Flaxseed Oil

**Omega-3 Preservation** — Flaxseed oil contains 50-60% alpha-linolenic acid (ALA), an essential omega-3 fatty acid. This compound is extremely heat-sensitive and begins to degrade above 50°C. Only cold pressing can preserve the full omega-3 content.

**Premium Market Position** — Cold-pressed flaxseed oil sells for $15-30 per liter in retail markets, significantly higher than commodity cooking oils.

**Growing Demand** — The global flaxseed oil market is growing at 12% CAGR, driven by increasing consumer awareness of omega-3 health benefits.

### Flaxseed Oil Production Data

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Oil Content | 33-43% |
| Cold Press Yield | 28-35% |
| Omega-3 (ALA) Content | 50-60% of oil |
| Optimal Press Temperature | Below 50°C |
| Shelf Life | 3-6 months (refrigerated) |
| Retail Price Range | $15-30/L |

### Key Applications

- **Health Supplements**: Omega-3 capsules and liquid supplements
- **Gourmet Cooking**: Finishing oil for salads and cold dishes (never for high-heat cooking)
- **Cosmetics**: Natural skin care and hair treatment products
- **Industrial**: Linseed oil for wood finishing and artist paints (hot-pressed)
