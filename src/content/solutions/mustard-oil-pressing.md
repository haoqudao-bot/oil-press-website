## Overview

Mustard oil is a staple cooking oil across South Asia — India, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Pakistan — where it is deeply embedded in culinary traditions and cultural practices. A dependable **mustard oil press machine** is the foundation of any mustard oil production business, whether you produce traditional Kachi Ghani cold-pressed oil or refined mustard oil for the mass market.

Haonuo's mustard oil pressing solution is specifically designed for the high-demand South Asian market. Our hydraulic **mustard oil extraction machine** delivers superior yield compared to traditional ghani (animal-driven) presses and conventional screw expellers, while preserving the characteristic pungent flavor that consumers expect from authentic mustard oil.

The solution supports both cold pressing (for premium Kachi Ghani mustard oil) and hot pressing with steaming (for higher yield and milder flavor), giving you the versatility to serve diverse market segments with a single production line.

## Oil Yield Expectations

| Raw Material | Oil Content | Expected Press Yield | Residual Oil Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow Mustard Seeds | 35-40% | 30-36% | ≤ 5% |
| Brown Mustard Seeds | 35-42% | 30-38% | ≤ 5% |
| Black Mustard Seeds | 35-40% | 28-35% | ≤ 6% |
| Steamed Mustard Seeds | 35-42% | 33-39% | ≤ 4% |

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Seed variety**: Brown mustard (Brassica juncea) typically yields more oil than black mustard
- **Steaming**: Pre-steaming increases yield by 3-5% and adjusts the pungency level
- **Moisture content**: Optimal pressing moisture is 5-7%
- **Pressing method**: Cold pressing (Kachi Ghani) yields slightly less but produces premium oil
