## Overview

Cottonseed oil is a major vegetable oil in West Africa, particularly Nigeria, which is Africa's largest cotton producer. With growing demand for affordable cooking oil in the region, cottonseed oil production presents a significant business opportunity.

Haonuo Machinery's cottonseed oil pressing solution is designed for the unique challenges of processing cottonseed — its high fiber content, the need for delinting, and the critical requirement to remove gossypol during refining. Our heavy-duty hydraulic press handles cottonseed efficiently, delivering high oil yield and quality.

## Oil Yield Expectations

| Raw Material | Oil Content | Expected Yield | Residual Oil in Cake |
|-------------|------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Whole cottonseed | 18-22% | 15-18% | 6-8% |
| Cottonseed kernels (decorticated) | 30-38% | 28-34% | 5-7% |
| Pre-pressed cake | 10-15% | 8-12% | 5-7% |

## Recommended Process

1. **Delinting** — Remove cotton lint using a delinting machine. This step is critical — lint absorbs 3-5% of your oil yield.
2. **Decortication** — Separate hulls from kernels. Hulls contain only 1-2% oil and reduce overall yield.
3. **Flaking** — Roll the kernels into thin flakes (0.3-0.5mm) to rupture cell walls and improve oil release.
4. **Hydraulic Pressing** — Load flakes into the HN355 Heavy Duty Hydraulic Press. Apply 63 MPa pressure for 30-40 minutes (hot press) or approximately 2 hours (cold press) per batch.
5. **Filtration** — Pass crude oil through the pneumatic filter press.
6. **Refining** — Critical step: degumming, neutralization, bleaching, and deodorization to remove gossypol and free fatty acids.
