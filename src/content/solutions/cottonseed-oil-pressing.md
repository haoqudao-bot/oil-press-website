  - question: Is cottonseed oil safe for consumption?

  - question: What is the oil yield from cottonseed?
    answer: Whole cottonseed yields approximately 15-20% oil. After decortication (removing hulls), cottonseed kernels yield 30-38% oil. Our hydraulic press achieves residual oil in cake below 7%.
  - question: Why do I need to remove the lint before pressing?
    answer: Cotton lint absorbs oil during pressing, reducing your yield by 3-5%. It also makes the pressed cake less valuable as animal feed. Delinting before pressing significantly improves both oil yield and cake quality.
  - question: Can I use the same machine for cottonseed and other oilseeds?
    answer: Yes, our hydraulic oil press is versatile and can process cottonseed, peanut, sesame, sunflower, and more. Simply adjust the pressure and temperature settings for each material.

**Quick Answer**

Yes — the Haonuo hydraulic oil press is built for cottonseed. Whole cottonseed yields **15-18%**; after delinting and decortication, kernels yield **28-34%**, with **residual oil in cake at 5-7%**. Cottonseed is almost always **hot-pressed** — cold pressing is not common for this seed. The critical preparation step: **delint before pressing** — cotton lint absorbs oil and costs you **3-5% of yield**. And remember crude cottonseed oil must be refined to remove gossypol before it can be sold as edible oil.
