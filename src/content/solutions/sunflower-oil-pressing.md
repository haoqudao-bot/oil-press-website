Sunflower oil is one of the most popular cooking oils in the world, dominating kitchens across Eastern Europe, Russia, Turkey, and increasingly in Africa and South America. A reliable **sunflower oil press machine** is essential for producers looking to capitalize on this growing global demand, whether for premium cold-pressed sunflower oil or high-volume refined cooking oil.

Haonuo's sunflower oil pressing solution uses hydraulic press technology that outperforms conventional screw expellers in both yield and oil quality. Our hydraulic sunflower oil press machine applies uniform high pressure that extracts more oil per kilogram of seeds while maintaining low processing temperatures that preserve the oil's natural vitamin E content and light, neutral flavor.

This solution handles both high-oil and confectionery sunflower seed varieties, with optional dehulling and roasting steps that let you optimize your production for different market requirements and price points.

**Key factors affecting yield:**
- **Seed variety**: High-oil varieties (45-50% oil content) yield significantly more than confectionery types
- **Dehulling**: Removing hulls can increase effective yield by 10-15% since hulls absorb oil
- **Roasting**: Pre-roasting at 120-150°C increases yield by 3-5%
- **Moisture content**: Optimal pressing moisture is 6-8%

## Recommended Process

1. **Seed Cleaning** — Remove field debris, stones, and damaged seeds. Clean seeds produce better oil and protect your equipment.
2. **Dehulling (Recommended)** — Remove sunflower seed hulls using our seed crusher. Dehulling increases yield by 10-15% and produces lighter-colored oil. For whole-seed pressing, skip this step.
3. **Roasting** — Roast dehulled kernels at 120-150°C for 15-25 minutes using the Thermal Oil Roasting Pan. This improves oil flow and yield. For cold-pressed oil, skip roasting.
4. **Hydraulic Pressing** — Feed prepared sunflower seeds into the Standard or Heavy Duty Hydraulic Oil Press. The 390mm barrel processes 100 kg per batch with an 8-15 minute cycle (varies by seed type).
5. **Filtration** — Pass crude sunflower oil through the Pneumatic Oil Filter Press for clear, market-ready oil.
6. **Refining (Optional)** — For refined sunflower oil, use the Edible Oil Refining System to produce light-colored, neutral-flavored cooking oil.
7. **Packaging** — Bottle the finished oil. Cold-pressed sunflower oil should be stored in dark containers to prevent oxidation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a sunflower oil press machine cost?**
Our sunflower oil press machines are available in various configurations from standard to heavy-duty models with double barrels. We offer factory-direct pricing with no middleman markup.

**Do I need to dehull sunflower seeds before pressing?**
Dehulling is strongly recommended. Sunflower seed hulls absorb 10-15% of the oil during pressing, significantly reducing your yield. Our seed crusher can handle dehulling efficiently. However, if dehulling equipment is not available, you can press whole seeds with a lower yield.

**What is the difference between cold-pressed and refined sunflower oil?**
Cold-pressed sunflower oil retains more vitamin E, phytosterols, and natural flavor. It has a golden color and nutty taste, commanding premium prices in health food markets. Refined sunflower oil is lighter in color, has a neutral taste, and a higher smoke point, making it more versatile for cooking.

**Which sunflower seed variety is best for oil pressing?**
High-oil varieties with 45-50% oil content are ideal. Popular pressing varieties include Peredovik, VNIIMK, and HAOL 414. Black-seeded varieties typically have higher oil content than striped confectionery types.
