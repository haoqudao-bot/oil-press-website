How the HN325 Works: Hot & Cold Pressing Workflows

Every Haonuo hydraulic press handles both methods — oil temperature is set by whether you roast the seed first, not by the machine.

Hot pressing (max yield)

  1. Clean & sort; dehull if needed
  2. Condition to 5–8% moisture
  3. Roast at 160–180°C, 25–35 min
  4. Crush, then press 100 kg @ full force, 30–40 min
  5. Filter 200–300 mesh; settle & store

Cold pressing (premium oil)

  1. Clean & color-sort
  2. Condition to 7–9% moisture
  3. Gentle crushing below 60°C
  4. Press ~2 hours
  5. Multi-stage fine filtration; store cool & dark

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a hydraulic oil press machine work?

The HN325 uses a hydraulic cylinder to press seed inside an enclosed barrel. Cleaned, conditioned (and usually roasted) seed is loaded in batches of up to 100 kg; the 325-ton ram applies static pressure — 27.78 MPa on the cake — for 30–40 minutes (hot) or about 2 hours (cold), squeezing oil through the barrel's slot walls. Static pressing adds no friction heat, so raw dried seed stays naturally below 60°C.

How much oil can I press per day?

With hot pressing and full 100 kg batches: roughly 900–1,000 kg per 8-hour shift with a single barrel, or 1,200–1,400 kg with the double-barrel setup. Cold pressing runs slower: about 300–400 kg (single) or 600–700 kg (double). Fluffy materials fill the barrel below 100 kg, so plan proportionally.

HN325 or HN365 — which should I choose?

Up to about a ton of hot-pressed seed per day, the double-barrel HN325 is enough. The HN365 (365 t, vertical, available in single or double station) adds force and cuts waiting time between cycles for larger volumes. Send us your seed type and daily target for a throughput calculation.

My budget is tight — is the older HN300 enough?

The HN300 remains in production and shares barrels and common wear parts with the HN325. Its shorter stroke suits smaller effective batches; if you want full 100 kg barrels pressed through, the HN325's 850 mm long stroke is built for it.

What seeds can it press?

All conventional oilseeds: sesame, peanut, rapeseed, soybean, sunflower, flaxseed, tea seed, cottonseed and more. Very hard in-shell materials such as walnut and apricot kernel are better matched to the higher-force HN426 or HN500.

What oil yield can I expect?

Factory-test ranges in hot pressing: sesame 42–54%, peanut 40–50%, rapeseed 35–45%. Cold pressing yields somewhat less but produces a lighter-tasting oil that sells at a premium. Results vary with seed variety, moisture and preparation.

What power supply does it need?

A 2.2 kW motor on ordinary 220V or 380V supply; it also runs on a 5 kVA generator where the grid is unreliable. We customize voltage to your local standard at no extra charge.

Can I get a stainless steel or custom barrel?

Yes — barrel diameter, height and food-grade stainless steel are available as custom options. Custom configurations take 30–45 days to build; standard ones 15–25 working days.
