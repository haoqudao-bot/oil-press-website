How the HN325 Works: Hot & Cold Pressing Workflows

Every Haonuo hydraulic press handles both methods — oil temperature is set by whether you roast the seed first, not by the machine.

Hot pressing (max yield)

  1. Clean & sort; dehull if needed
  2. Condition to 5–8% moisture
  3. Roast at 160–180°C, 25–35 min
  4. Crush, then press 100 kg @ 60 MPa, 30–40 min
  5. Filter 200–300 mesh; settle & store

Cold pressing (premium oil)

  1. Clean & color-sort
  2. Condition to 7–9% moisture
  3. Gentle crushing below 60°C
  4. Press ~2 hours
  5. Multi-stage fine filtration; store cool & dark

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a hydraulic oil press machine work?

The HN325 uses a hydraulic cylinder to press seed inside an enclosed barrel at up to 60 MPa. Cleaned, conditioned (and usually roasted) seed is loaded in 100 kg batches; the 325-ton ram applies static pressure for 30–40 minutes (hot) or about 2 hours (cold), squeezing oil through the barrel's slot walls. Static pressing adds no friction heat, so raw dried seed stays naturally below 60°C.

HN325 or HN365 — which should I choose?

Up to about 1 ton of seed per day, the double-barrel HN325 is enough: you load one barrel while the other presses. The HN365's two independent vertical stations remove waiting time more completely for larger volumes. Send us your seed type and daily target for a throughput calculation.

My budget is tight — is the older HN300 enough?

The HN300 remains in production and shares barrels and common wear parts with the HN325. It gives up 25 tons of force and the 850 mm extended stroke. Below ~800 kg of seed per day, most mills find it sufficient.

What oil yield can I expect?

Factory-test ranges: sesame 42–54%, peanut 40–50%, rapeseed 35–45% in hot pressing. Cold pressing yields somewhat less but produces a lighter-tasting oil that sells at a premium. Results vary with seed variety, moisture and preparation.

Can it press coconut, walnut or other hard materials?

The HN325 is built for conventional oilseeds. For hard-shell or cold-press-focused production (coconut, walnut, apricot kernel), the higher-pressure HN426 (500 t, 70 MPa) is the right machine.

What power supply does it need?

2.2 kW motor on ordinary 220V or 380V supply; it also runs on a 5 kVA generator where the grid is unreliable. We customize voltage to your local standard at no extra charge.

Can I get a stainless steel or custom barrel?

Yes — barrel diameter, height and food-grade stainless steel are available as custom options. Custom configurations take 30–45 days to build; standard ones 15–25 working days.

How is the machine shipped?

Sea freight as standard; a single-barrel HN325 fits a 1200 × 1200 mm footprint with 2.5 m ceiling clearance. Common spare parts ship by DHL/FedEx in 5–7 days to most destinations. Customs time is additional.
